## The figure this cannot see

    Studio > Earn > Eligibility shows the qualified Shorts views and
    qualified watch hours YouTube actually counts. The API exposes
    neither. On FaRu Fact on 2026-10-14 that panel read 3.6K qualified
    Shorts views where the public count was 14,639, and 8 qualified
    watch hours. Treat the panel as the truth and everything here as
    an upper bound.

From 1 February 2027 the ad-revenue thresholds rise for new applicants. The fan-funding ones do not, which is why it is the tier to aim at.
