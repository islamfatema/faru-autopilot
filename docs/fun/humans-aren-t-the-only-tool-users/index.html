Humans Aren't The Only Tool-Users!

Published 2026-09-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Humans are not the only tool-users! You probably learned that's our unique trait. But many animals make and use tools. New Caledonian crows craft hooked sticks, To pull out tasty insects from tight spots. Sea otters use rocks as little anvils. Cracking open hard shellfish on their chests. Even elephants use branches to swat flies. Or to scratch an itchy, unreachable spot. Showing real problem solving and cleverness. What's the cleverest tool-use you've seen?
