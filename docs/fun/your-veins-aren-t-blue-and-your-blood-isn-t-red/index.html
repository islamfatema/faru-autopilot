Your Veins Aren't Blue, And Your Blood Isn't Red!

Published 2026-09-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You see your veins look blue or green. And you might think your blood is blue. Or that it's always bright, cherry red. But neither is true. That's not the color. Blood, when it lacks oxygen, is a dark maroon, almost brown. It looks blue in your veins because light scatters differently. Your skin absorbs red light, making the darker blood appear blue. The blue you see is an optical illusion. So try it: pinch your skin to see your veins. They're not blue. What else do your eyes make you believe?
