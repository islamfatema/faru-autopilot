Why Coffee Makes You Feel Awake

Published 2026-10-27 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe coffee gives you energy. It's a natural boost of alertness. But coffee doesn't actually create energy. It works by masking fatigue signals. Your body is still tired, you just don't know. Caffeine blocks adenosine, a natural sleep chemical. This chemical builds up through the day. Telling your brain it is time to sleep. So you feel alert, but your reserves are low. This can lead to accumulated sleep debt. Your body still needs rest to function correctly. Is that alertness true energy, or a trick?
