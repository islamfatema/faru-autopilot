Why Ice Cream Gives You Headaches

Published 2026-09-19 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think brain freeze means your brain is cold. That sharp pain comes from eating cold treats. But your brain itself isn't actually freezing. The sensation is a reaction in your mouth. It's not direct brain pain you are feeling. Cold food chills vessels on your mouth's roof. They constrict then rapidly dilate to warm up. This sudden change triggers your trigeminal nerve. Your brain misinterprets this signal as pain in forehead. It's a protective reflex to make you slow down. This encourages you to eat treats more slowly. Is your body trying to make you savor it?
