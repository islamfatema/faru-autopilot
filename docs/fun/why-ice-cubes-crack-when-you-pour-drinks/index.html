Why Ice Cubes Crack When You Pour Drinks

Published 2026-11-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

We often hear ice crack in our drinks. It's a common sound in your glass. But that cracking sound is not random. It is a direct result of rapid changes. Your ice experiences intense thermal stress. When warm liquid hits ice, it causes thermal shock. The outside layer heats and expands instantly. This expansion creates immense internal pressure. This rapid change weakens the ice structure. It makes your ice melt faster too. Understanding this helps you keep drinks colder. Does this physics lesson change how you chill drinks?
