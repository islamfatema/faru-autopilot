Spiders Can Hear You From Across the Room!

Published 2026-09-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume spiders are completely deaf today. You think they only feel ground vibrations nearby. That belief is entirely wrong since they hear. Spiders have hidden ears on their long legs. Specialized sensory hairs called trichobothria exist. These tiny hairs detect airborne sound waves. Studies prove jumping spiders react three meters away. This means they hear your footsteps approaching. They use this hidden hearing to hunt prey. Air pressure moves these hairs to trigger nerves. They listen to your room without any eardrums. Could you sleep knowing spiders hear you whispering?
