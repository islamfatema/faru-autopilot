Why Your Stomach Growls When You're Hungry!

Published 2026-09-30 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You hear your stomach growl when you are hungry. You think it is demanding food right now. But that familiar rumble isn't always about hunger. Your digestive system is always hard at work. It's a continuous process moving food, liquid, and gas. These sounds, called borborygmi, are constantly happening inside. Your gut muscles contract at least every 2-3 seconds. This moves contents through your 25-foot long intestines. So that rumble means your body is always active. It's efficiently processing, or getting ready to process food. Meaning your body is working even when you don't notice. Does knowing this make you feel more connected to your gut?
