Teeth Are Not Bones

Published 2026-09-30 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Your body cannot regrow lost tooth enamel. In 2010 biologists classified tooth types. Broken bones mend while teeth stay broken. Damage to your enamel is permanent forever.
