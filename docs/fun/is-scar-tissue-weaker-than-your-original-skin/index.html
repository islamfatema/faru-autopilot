Is Scar Tissue Weaker Than Your Original Skin?

Published 2026-11-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume once a wound heals into a scar, your skin is 'good as new,' fully repaired. But that fundamental assumption is wrong: Scar tissue is not as strong as your original skin. Why wouldn't your body heal itself perfectly? Scar tissue is made of collagen fibers laid down in a less organized, crisscrossed pattern, not basket-weave. This makes scar tissue only 70 to 80 percent as strong as your original skin and much less elastic. Try this: Gently pinch a scar on your body Now pinch unscarred skin. What difference do you feel?
