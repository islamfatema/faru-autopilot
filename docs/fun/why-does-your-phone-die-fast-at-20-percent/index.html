Why Does Your Phone Die Fast At 20 Percent?

Published 2026-11-11 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Once voltage dips low, the math formula panics. Dropping five percent in a single real minute. Try it yourself: restart at five percent. Watch the battery number jump back up to fifteen.
