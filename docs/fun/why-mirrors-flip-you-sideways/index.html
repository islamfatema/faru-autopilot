Why Mirrors Flip You Sideways

Published 2026-11-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Everyone assumes mirrors flip left and right. Mirrors actually flip front and back. Your reflection is a front-to-back reversal. It mimics another person facing toward you. Your left side stays on the left. Your reflection is facing away from you. Our brains interpret this as a sideways swap. We expect objects to be fully rotated. Light reflects along a perpendicular axis. This optical confusion tricks daily awareness. Why do humans trust faulty mirror reflections? Physics reveals the surprising truth.
