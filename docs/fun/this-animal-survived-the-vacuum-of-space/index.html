This Animal Survived the Vacuum of Space

Published 2026-09-14 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

This animal survived the vacuum of space. It's a tardigrade. A water bear. Smaller than a grain of salt. In 2007, scientists sent dried tardigrades into orbit - outside the spacecraft. Ten days of vacuum. Back on Earth, they added water. The ones shielded from the Sun's radiation survived about as well as ones that never left. Would you trust a tardigrade to survive the apocalypse? Fact number 9: you are shorter right now than when you woke up.
