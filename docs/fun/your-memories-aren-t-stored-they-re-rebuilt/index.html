Your Memories Aren't Stored, They're Rebuilt!

Published 2026-09-11 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Your memories aren't stored, they're rebuilt. You think they're like fixed files, saved away. But every single time you recall a memory, your brain doesn't just retrieve it. It actively reconstructs it on the spot. This makes memories vulnerable to changes, and new information each time. It's why eyewitness testimony can be so unreliable, even with good intent. And why your own past feels like a story you're always refining, without knowing it. Try to recall a vivid, old memory right now. Does it feel exactly the same as last time?
