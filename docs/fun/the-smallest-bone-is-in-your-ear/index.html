The Smallest Bone Is In Your Ear

Published 2026-09-26 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your smallest bone is in your finger. That belief is wrong because it is elsewhere. The smallest bone lives deep inside your ear. It is called the stapes or the stirrup bone. Measuring three millimeters, it beats a grain of rice. It amplifies vibrations from your outer eardrum. Microscopic movements let you hear everyday sounds. Your hearing relies on this tiny moving lever. Sound waves depend on this minuscule body part. Without this tiny bone you would hear nothing. Why is our hearing tied to such fragile structures?
