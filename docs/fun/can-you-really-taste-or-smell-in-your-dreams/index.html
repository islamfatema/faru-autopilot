Can You Really Taste Or Smell In Your Dreams?

Published 2026-11-04 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume dreams are full sensory experiences. Like being awake, seeing and hearing everything. But your brain almost never creates tastes or smells. While visual parts are active during REM sleep, the areas for taste and smell are largely suppressed. You can eat a feast in your dream, but no real taste. Your brain only recalls the *idea* of eating. Two entire senses are almost absent from dreams. Try recalling a dream where you tasted something specific. It's almost impossible to find even one memory. What other senses are missing from your dream world?
