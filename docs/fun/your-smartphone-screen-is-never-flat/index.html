Your Smartphone Screen Is Never Flat

Published 2026-09-24 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe your phone screen is totally flat. A smooth, rigid glass plane in your hand. That belief ignores how glass actually acts. Your screen bows inward every time you tap it. Millions of microscopic bends happen daily. The glass flexes down without ever snapping. Try this right now: Turn off your screen. Press your thumb firmly while watching a lamp. Watch the reflection warp as the glass bends. So how does the display survive years of force?
