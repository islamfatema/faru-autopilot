This Tree Was Alive Before the Great Pyramid Was Finished

Published 2026-09-12 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

This tree was alive before the Great Pyramid was finished. It's a bristlecone pine in California. People call it Methuselah. Its rings put it at about 4,850 years old. The Great Pyramid was finished around 2560 BC - by then, this tree was already centuries old. It's still alive. On a mountain, right now, growing. What's the oldest thing you've ever touched?
