Look at a Clock - The First Second Freezes

Published 2026-09-16 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Look at a clock. The first second will freeze. That tick takes too long. You're not imagining it. When your eyes jump, the world blurs. Your brain hides that - and fills the gap by stretching what you land on backwards in time. It's called chronostasis. Try it on the next clock you see. Then look back here. Did it freeze for you? Yes or no. That's the first 14. Week two starts right after - and number 15 sounds the most fake yet.
