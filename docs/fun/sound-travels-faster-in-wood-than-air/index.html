Sound Travels Faster in Wood Than Air

Published 2026-09-06 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Sound travels faster through a solid table than it does through the open air. You think heavy walls block sound because they are meant to stop the noise. But solids are actually highway lanes for waves. Air is a terrible conductor compared to wood. Sound moves at three hundred meters per second in air, but flies through pine wood at over three thousand. That is nearly ten times faster. Try it yourself: tap a table lightly.
