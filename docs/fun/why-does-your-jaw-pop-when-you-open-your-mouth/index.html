Why Does Your Jaw Pop When You Open Your Mouth?

Published 2026-10-28 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your jaw pops because bones grind together? That painful idea is completely false. Your jawbones never touch when you open your mouth. A tiny soft cartilage disc sits right between them. It acts as a shock absorber for every single bite. When that loose disc slips, you hear an audible snap. Your skull is catching a sliding piece of tissue. Most people have a slight disc slip every single day. Try placing your fingers on your jaw joints right now. Feel that tiny bump snap underneath your fingertips.
