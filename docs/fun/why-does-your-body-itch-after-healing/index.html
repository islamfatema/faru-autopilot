Why Does Your Body Itch After Healing?

Published 2026-10-29 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

They fire off random electrical signals. Your brain receives them and gets totally confused. It translates those signals directly into an itch. Tricking you into scratching a fragile healing spot.
