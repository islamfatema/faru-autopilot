Why Does Your Left Eye See Colors Differently?

Published 2026-10-20 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

One eye leans toward warmer reds and yellows. The other captures cooler blues and greens. Your brain blends them into one composite view. Cover one eye and look at a white wall. Switch eyes instantly to test this right now. You will notice a distinct color shift immediately. If your eyes disagree, which one is lying?
