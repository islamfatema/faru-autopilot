Why Red Lights Preserve Your Night Vision

Published 2026-10-19 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You know red lights help preserve night vision. Astronauts and submariners depend on this principle. But it's not just a simple light trick. Your eyes have specific rods for dim light. These rods contain rhodopsin a light-sensitive pigment. Rhodopsin takes around 30 minutes to fully regenerate. Other light colors rapidly break down this rhodopsin. But red light at 600nm or more, barely affects it. This means your night vision remains active and ready. You can still see details without losing adaptation. It’s why these specific lights are so essential. Should public spaces use more red light at night?
