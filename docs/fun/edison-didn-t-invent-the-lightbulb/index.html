Edison Didn't Invent the Lightbulb!

Published 2026-09-04 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Edison gets credit for inventing the lightbulb. But that's not entirely true. The first electric light was made in 1802. Humphry Davy created an electric arc lamp. Later, Joseph Swan had working prototypes. These were before Edison's 1879 patent. Edison didn't invent the lightbulb itself. He vastly improved it to be practical for homes. He developed a long-lasting filament and system. So, is invention about discovery or usefulness?
