Why Do Your Hands Wrinkle When You Hold Snow?

Published 2026-10-26 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your fingers wrinkle from cold water. Most people assume it is just normal water absorption. That is not what is happening at all. It is an active nervous system survival response. Freezing temperatures force blood vessels to constrict. Causing the overlying skin tissue to shrink and fold. This evolutionary design actually improves your grip. Helps you hold onto wet, slippery ice objects firmly. Try grabbing a handful of ice cubes right now. Watch your fingertips pucker within thirty seconds.
