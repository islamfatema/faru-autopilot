Are You Taller In The Morning Than At Night?

Published 2026-11-08 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume your height is the same all day, a fixed number on a tape measure, always. But that common belief is completely wrong: You are taller in the morning than at night. How can your height change without you knowing? Throughout the day, gravity compresses your spinal discs. These soft discs, which are full of fluid, get squashed. You can 'shrink' by up to half an an inch. When you sleep, these discs rehydrate and expand. Try this: Measure your height right after waking. Then measure again just before you go to bed.
