Your Brain Can't Actually Feel Pain!

Published 2026-09-10 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think a headache means your brain hurts? Everyone believes the brain is the source of its own pain. But the actual brain tissue has no pain receptors! How can the organ that processes all pain not feel it itself? It sounds impossible. Headaches come from nerves, blood vessels, and muscles *around* the brain, not the brain itself. Neurosurgeons can operate on an awake patient's brain, and they won't feel the brain being touched. Next time you feel a headache, realize it's surrounding tissue.
