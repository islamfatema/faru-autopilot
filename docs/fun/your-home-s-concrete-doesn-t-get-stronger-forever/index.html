Your Home's Concrete Doesn't Get Stronger Forever!

Published 2026-09-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably think the concrete in your home just gets stronger forever. That once it's set, it's indestructible and will never fail. But that's not true. It's a common, and dangerous, misconception. Concrete hardens for a few years, then that process stops. After 25 to 50 years, modern concrete starts to degrade. It's weakened by freezing, thawing, chemicals, and constant stress. Which is why old sidewalks and foundations start to crack and crumble. Engineers know its lifespan is limited, and plan for replacement. So, try it: look at an old concrete driveway. See the cracks?
