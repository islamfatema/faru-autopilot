The Platypus Has No Stomach

Published 2026-09-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

This animal has no stomach. A platypus. Its food goes from its throat almost straight into its intestine. Somewhere in its evolution it lost the genes that make stomach acid and digestive enzymes. Scientists found them deleted or switched off in its DNA. And it's not alone. About a quarter of fish species don't have a true stomach either. Which body part could you live without?
