Why Does Food Taste Bad When You're Sick?

Published 2026-10-24 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think being sick dulls your taste buds. You assume your tongue stops working properly. Your tongue is actually working perfectly fine. Your taste buds only detect five basic tastes. Eighty percent of flavor comes from your nose. When sickness blocks your nasal passage, flavor molecules cannot reach olfactory nerves. This leaves you with just raw salt and sugar. Try it yourself—pinch your nose tightly now, then eat an apple slice or a raw onion piece. You cannot tell them apart until you let go! What other everyday sense is actually an illusion?
