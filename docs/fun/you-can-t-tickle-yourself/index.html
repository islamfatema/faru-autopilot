You Can't Tickle Yourself

Published 2026-09-20 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe tickling is just about touch. A simple physical reaction to stimulation. But try to tickle yourself right now. Go on, try it. It just does not work. Your brain knows it is you, and anticipates. It cancels surprise, the key to ticklishness. The cerebellum predicts and dulls the sensation. In 1998 researchers proved this brain filter. Your nervous system blocks your own touch. You cannot surprise yourself with tickles. This keeps your mind focused on outside threats. What other sensations does your brain hide?
