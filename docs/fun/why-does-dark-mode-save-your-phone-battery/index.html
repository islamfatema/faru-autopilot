Why Does Dark Mode Save Your Phone Battery?

Published 2026-11-10 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think dark mode saves battery by dimming lights. You assume a backlight just shines through dark glass. That backlight idea is completely outdated. Modern OLED phone screens have no central backlight. Every single pixel is an independent tiny light. Over 2 million pixels power themselves directly. When displaying pure black, those pixels turn fully off. They draw zero power from your battery. Dark mode reduces screen power by up to 47 percent. Try it right now: open a black picture in the dark. The black pixels emit absolutely zero light. Why does anyone still use bright light mode?
