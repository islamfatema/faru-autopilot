Do Your Bones Constantly Rebuild Themselves?

Published 2026-11-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your adult bones are solid, unchanging. Permanent scaffolding that just stays put forever. That belief is a profound misunderstanding of your body. Your bones are living, dynamic tissues inside you. They constantly break down and rebuild themselves. This process, called remodeling, happens non-stop. Old bone is removed by osteoclasts, new by osteoblasts. Your entire skeleton is replaced every ten years. This keeps bones strong, repairs damage, adapts to stress. It’s why a broken bone can heal so effectively and fully. Try it: remember your body from ten years ago. Your skeleton is completely different now than it was then.
