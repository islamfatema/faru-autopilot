Why Does Your Mouth Burn After Eating Pineapple?

Published 2026-11-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Think pineapple acidity makes your tongue sting? You assume fruit acid attacks your soft tissue. That stinging belief is completely wrong. Raw pineapple contains a powerful protein-eating enzyme. It is called bromelain, and it targets living flesh. As you chew your snack, it digests your mouth lining. Your body races to repair the microscopic damage fast. That ongoing tissue attack causes the raw sensation. Try cooking the fruit first to destroy the enzymes entirely. What is your favorite way to eat fresh pineapple?
