Your Nose Does Most Of Your Tasting

Published 2026-09-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe your tongue does all of your tasting. That your taste buds are responsible for flavor. But your tongue only tastes five basic things. Sweet, sour, salty, bitter, and umami. The complex richness you experience? That is almost entirely your sense of smell. As you eat, odor molecules travel to your nose. They create the intricate tapestry of flavor. Try this: Pinch your nose completely shut. Now bite into an apple. It tastes bland, right? Release your nose. What other truths are hidden by a single variable?
