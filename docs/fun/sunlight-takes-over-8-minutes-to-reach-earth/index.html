Sunlight Takes Over 8 Minutes to Reach Earth

Published 2026-10-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Specifically, it takes 8 minutes and 20 seconds. Light travels at an incredible 300,000 km/second. Even at this speed, distance makes a delay. The Sun is 150 million kilometers from Earth. This means you are always seeing the past. If the Sun vanished we wouldn't know for 8 minutes. Your present is actually the Sun's history. What else in the universe is a delay?
