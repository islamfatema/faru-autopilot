Why Do Cats Knead You?

Published 2026-09-28 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You might think your cat is making biscuits... ...because they loved their mother's milk. But that's not the full scientific story at all. It is a behavior they carry from kittenhood, yes, but the reason they do it as adults is different. Your feline friend has scent glands in their paws. Kneading actively activates these tiny scent markers. It's a primal territorial instinct for marking you. It means they are literally claiming you as theirs. It also shows deep trust and genuine affection. They feel safe enough to make you their property. So, is being 'marked' a good thing for you?
