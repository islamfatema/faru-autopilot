Does Your Skin Actually Measure Temperature?

Published 2026-11-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your skin is a precise thermometer. Accurately telling you the exact temperature of things. You assume your body measures absolute degrees like a machine. But the truth is, your skin is terrible at measuring that. Its job is to detect *changes* in temperature, not absolutes. Your thermoreceptors compare current temp to recent ones. This is why you can fool your skin so easily and often. If you put hands in hot and cold, then lukewarm... Your hands will disagree if it's hot or cold water. Try this: get three bowls, hot, cold, and lukewarm. Then put both hands in the lukewarm; they will disagree. What other senses trick you every single day?
