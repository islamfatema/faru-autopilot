Why Stars Look Pointy to Your Eye

Published 2026-09-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think stars look pointy due to shape. That common belief is actually wrong. This visual effect is called diffraction spiking. Starlight enters your eye and interacts with obstacles. Dust particles and edges bend the light waves. Interference patterns create spiky points in vision. This optical illusion happens inside your own eye. You never see true stellar shapes directly. This means your vision distorts distant starlight. Your own eyelashes help create the bright points. Human optics completely alter what you perceive above. Why trust your eyes when they trick you?
