Are You More Bacteria Than Human Cells?

Published 2026-11-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe your body is entirely human cells, a single organism, pure and completely distinct. But your fundamental belief is completely wrong: You are actually a walking, breathing ecosystem. How can something so small outnumber your own cells? Recent science shows you carry roughly 1 bacterial cell for every 1 human cell, on your skin and in your gut. These microbes aren't just hitchhiking - they're vital for your digestion, and your immune system. Try this: Picture your skin. Imagine it covered in trillions of microbes.
