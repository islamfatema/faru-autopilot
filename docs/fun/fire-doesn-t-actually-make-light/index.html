Fire Doesn't Actually Make Light!

Published 2026-09-04 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think fire makes its own light? It looks like the flames themselves are glowing. But the fire itself is not making the light. It is the superheated materials in the flame. Tiny carbon particles and gases glow intensely. They are heated by the combustion reaction. This is called incandescence. A perfectly clean flame would be almost invisible. So it's not the fire, it's what the fire heats. What other phenomena are you misinterpreting?
