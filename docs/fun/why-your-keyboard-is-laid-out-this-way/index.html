Why Your Keyboard Is Laid Out This Way

Published 2026-09-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your keyboard layout speeds up typing. That common belief is totally wrong. It was actually made to slow you down. Early mechanical typewriters used metal arms. In eighteen seventy three, keys jammed constantly. Fast fingers made adjacent arms collide. Common letters were separated on purpose. Inventors forced typists to slow down. Your typing speed is permanently limited. Every email you send takes longer today. Your thumbs adapt to flawed history. Why accept an old mechanical design flaw?
