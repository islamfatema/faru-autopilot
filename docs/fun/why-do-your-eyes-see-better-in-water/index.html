Why Do Your Eyes See Better In Water?

Published 2026-10-31 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think water ruins your vision underwater. Making everything a blurry mess. That is backwards. Water isn't the problem. Light bends as it moves from air to eye. Your cornea does most of that bending. Water has a density close to your cornea. So light barely bends when you submerge. Without that bend, you cannot focus light. Your eye needs air to bend light correctly. Try opening your eyes in a pool next time. What else do your eyes need air to do?
