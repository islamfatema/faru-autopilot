How Much Is Your Body Worth If Sold for Elements?

Published 2026-10-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably assume your living body is worth a fortune on the open market. That common idea is wrong because chemistry strips away your value entirely. If you take an adult human body apart completely into its basic chemical ingredients, you get ten pounds of carbon, four pounds of nitrogen, and some iron. Packaged up and sold to chemical supply companies, those raw elements total less than ten dollars today. Yet that same collection of cheap basic dust manages to build a fully living, thinking person. Try it yourself - look at your hands right now and realize they are made of cheap elements.
