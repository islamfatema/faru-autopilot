High Heels Were Invented for Male Soldiers

Published 2026-09-06 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

High heels were originally designed for male soldiers. You think they were made for women's fashion, but they were actually essential combat gear. It seems impossible since they are so fragile, but they were never meant for the ground. In the tenth century, Persian cavalry wore high wooden heels to lock into stirrups. This allowed them to stand up securely and shoot their bows with deadly accuracy. Try it yourself: stand on your tiptoes. Feel how your calf muscles lock up tightly. That is the exact stance soldiers used on horses. Send this to someone who wears heels today.
