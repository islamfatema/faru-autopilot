Hot Water Can Freeze Faster Than Cold Water!

Published 2026-09-08 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Hot water can freeze faster than cold water! You were taught that cold always freezes first. But sometimes, physics defies common sense. This is known as the Mpemba effect. Scientists are still debating why it happens. It could be faster evaporation reducing mass, or differences in dissolved gases, or how heat moves through the water. It's a real paradox of phase change! Fill two ice trays: hot water in one, cold in another. Put them in your freezer and see what happens! What other science rules are more flexible?
