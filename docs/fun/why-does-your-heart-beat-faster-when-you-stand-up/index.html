Why Does Your Heart Beat Faster When You Stand Up?

Published 2026-10-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably think getting out of bed requires no extra effort from your circulation. That effortless view is completely wrong because standing up forces gravity to pull blood down to your feet. Your brain detects this sudden pressure drop in mere milliseconds. It commands your heart to beat faster preventing you from fainting. Try lying flat on the floor and jumping straight up to feel your pulse race. How many times a day does your heart save you?
