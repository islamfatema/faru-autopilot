The Moon Doesn't Change Size!

Published 2026-09-08 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

When the moon is low, it looks HUGE. Almost like it's closer to you. But this is not physically true. The moon is always the same size. It's a brain trick, an optical illusion. Your brain thinks horizon objects are farther away, so it makes it appear bigger, to keep its size consistent with what you know. Try it yourself: next full moon, use your thumb at arm's length to measure it when it's high and low. You'll see it is always the same!
