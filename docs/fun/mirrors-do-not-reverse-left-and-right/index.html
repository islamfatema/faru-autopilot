Mirrors Do Not Reverse Left and Right

Published 2026-09-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Mirrors do not actually reverse left-to-right. You think your reflection is flipped horizontally, but your mirror is not switching your hands. It does something much stranger instead. It reverses your body front-to-back. This seems impossible because your reflection looks like a real person standing in front of you. But if you point north, the reflection points north. Your left hand stays on the left side. Try it yourself: point to your right. Your reflection points to the exact same wall, not the opposite one. It only flipped your depth.
