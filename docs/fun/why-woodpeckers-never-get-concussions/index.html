Why Woodpeckers Never Get Concussions

Published 2026-11-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Woodpeckers slam their beaks into solid trees. The force of these impacts is immense. You'd expect them to suffer serious concussions. Yet, they completely avoid any kind of brain injury. This isn't just luck; it's unique anatomy. Their specialized skull acts as a shock absorber. It contains dense, spongy bone to cushion impacts. A long hyoid bone wraps around their skull. Like an internal seatbelt, it holds the brain tight. This incredible design shows nature's perfect engineering. Could we apply these principles to human safety?
