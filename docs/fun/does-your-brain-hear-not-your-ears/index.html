Does Your Brain Hear, Not Your Ears?

Published 2026-10-26 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your ears are where you hear? Most people believe the sound forms there. But your ears are just collecting vibrations. They only convert sound into electrical signals. It is your brain that actually processes it. Your brain filters noise and locates the source. It even fills in details your ears might miss. Try this: hum loudly and block your ears tight. You still hear your voice through your own bones. What other senses are actually in your brain?
