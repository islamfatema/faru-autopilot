Why You Get Zapped By Static Electricity

Published 2026-09-26 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You might think static shocks are just random zaps. Maybe you believe you're just 'charged up.' But it's not a random magic spark. It is a predictable transfer of invisible charge. Your body simply gains or loses tiny electrons. When materials like shoes rub on carpet, charges transfer. This creates an imbalance of electric charges on you. Touching a metal doorknob completes an invisible circuit. This means your body becomes a walking battery now. You store potential energy waiting for a path to discharge. So the zap is just physics evening things out. Do you know how to minimize these annoying shocks?
