Your Brain Sees, Not Your Eyes!

Published 2026-09-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your eyes just show you the world? Most people believe they are like perfect cameras. But your eyes only send raw, incomplete signals. They have gaps, and even blind spots. It's your brain that actively constructs vision. It takes that data and fills in the blanks. Using memory and what it expects to see. What you 'see' is a model your brain built. Not a direct feed of what's truly out there. How much of your reality is a sophisticated hallucination?
