Watermelon Is Actually a Berry

Published 2026-10-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think you know exactly what a berry is. Strawberries are surely a classic, obvious example. But botanists say that strawberries are not berries. And guess what: raspberries aren't berries either. Yet, watermelons, bananas, and avocados ARE berries. A true berry develops from a single flower ovary. And contains all fleshy layers, not just seeds. This botanical classification is surprisingly precise. So your fruit salad might contain more berries. Or fewer, depending on your botanical knowledge. It fundamentally changes how you see produce.
