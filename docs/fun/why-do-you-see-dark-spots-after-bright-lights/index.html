Why Do You See Dark Spots After Bright Lights?

Published 2026-10-29 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Look at a bright camera flash and you see a floating dark spot. You believe the intense light burned a shadow on your eye. You think that dark blob is physical damage to retina. Your eye was not burned at all. Bright light rapidly drains chemical pigments in your eye. Those overworked light sensors temporarily shut down completely. They send a signal of absolute blackness straight to your brain. You aren't seeing a burn mark; you see tired sensors resting. Try it yourself: look at a lamp, close your eyes, and blink fast... ...and watch the dark spot flip into a bright glowing ghost. How long does your brain take to reset after sudden light?
