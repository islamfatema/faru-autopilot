Horseshoe Crabs Are Older Than Trees

Published 2026-09-12 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

This animal is older than trees. Not one tree. Trees, as a thing that exists. Horseshoe crab fossils go back 445 million years. The first forests showed up about 385 million years ago. Sixty million years of crabs on a planet with no trees. And they're still on our beaches. What else do you think was around before trees?
