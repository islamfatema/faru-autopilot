Cold Does Not Actually Exist

Published 2026-09-04 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe cold is a thing, just like heat is a thing. We talk about 'feeling the cold' or 'adding cold' to something. But cold, in a physical sense, does not exist. Cold is simply the absence of heat. Heat is energy, the movement of molecules in matter. When something feels 'cold', it's losing heat energy. You don't add cold; you remove heat. Try this: Hold one hand in ice water for a minute. Hold your other hand in warm water for a minute. Then put both hands into lukewarm water. The 'cold' hand feels warm, the 'warm' hand feels cold. What else do you believe is real, that is only an absence?
