Your Skin Cannot Actually Feel Wetness

Published 2026-09-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Your skin cannot actually feel wetness. You think you feel water touching you, but humans have zero receptors for moisture. We are completely blind to wetness itself. Your brain has to hallucinate the feeling. This seems impossible since you feel wet daily. Your skin only detects temperature and friction. It combines rapid cold with smooth touch to guess that there is water on you. Try it yourself: wear a plastic glove and dip your hand in cold water. You will feel wet even though you are dry. Who else is living in a sensory illusion?
