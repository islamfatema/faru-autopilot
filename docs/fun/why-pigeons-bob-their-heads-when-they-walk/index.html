Why Pigeons Bob Their Heads When They Walk

Published 2026-10-30 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Pigeons bob their heads for a steady walking rhythm. Biological research shows an optical purpose. Pigeons have sideways eyes with wide peripheral vision. Continuous body movement blurs their field of view. They thrust their head forward to lock onto details. They freeze it to capture absolutely clear images. This constant pausing means they view the world as a series of jerky still photographs. Your fast moving surroundings look very different to birds.
