Is Your Phone Screen Made Of Normal Glass?

Published 2026-11-06 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your phone screen is just normal glass. Like a windowpane, easily shattered, very fragile. That belief is completely wrong and quite dangerous. Your phone screen is chemically strengthened glass. It's far from normal glass, it's engineered. It's soaked in hot potassium salt solution for hours. Smaller ions are swapped for larger ones, creating tension. This "ion exchange" forms a hard, compressed surface. That's why your screen is so resistant to everyday cracks. It can take bumps that would instantly spiderweb normal glass. Try it: scratch an old window with a metal key. Then, gently tap your phone screen with the same key.
