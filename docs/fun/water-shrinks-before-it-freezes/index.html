Water Shrinks Before It Freezes!

Published 2026-09-04 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You know water expands when it freezes? Most people think it just keeps expanding as it cools. But what happens right before freezing is stranger. Water contracts as it cools from room temperature. It gets denser, like most other liquids. But only until it hits exactly 4 degrees Celsius. Below 4°C, water begins to expand *again*. Then expands further when it turns to ice. This is why ice floats and lakes freeze top down. What other simple substances hide secrets?
