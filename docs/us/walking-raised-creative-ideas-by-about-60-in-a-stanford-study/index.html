Walking Raised Creative Ideas by About 60% in a Stanford Study

Published 2026-09-12 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Stuck on a problem? Stand up. Stanford researchers asked people to invent new uses for ordinary objects. Some sat. Some walked, even on a treadmill facing a blank wall. The walkers came up with about 60 percent more creative ideas. And the boost stayed when they sat back down. Your next good idea might be one walk away. Where do your best ideas show up - shower, walk, or 3 a.m.? number 5: what 17 hours awake does to your brain. It's been compared to alcohol.
