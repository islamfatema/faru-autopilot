Your Inner Critic Is Not Helping You

Published 2026-09-06 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your inner critic is keeping you sharp. You believe its harsh voice makes you work harder. But that voice is not your ally, it's a cage. It doesn't push you forward, it keeps you small. It feeds on doubt, not true potential. It paralyses action with fear of failure. It convinces you that you are not enough. Today, notice when it speaks, then gently reframe it. Turn "you can't do this" into "I will try this." Your growth begins when you choose kindness. What lie are you ready to stop believing?
