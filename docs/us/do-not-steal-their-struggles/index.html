Do Not Steal Their Struggles

Published 2026-10-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

People believe rescuing others is the ultimate love. Developmental research proves obstacles build resilience. When you rush to solve problems for everyone, You rob them of the friction they need. This means your constant interference creates dependence. Some essential lessons can only be learned alone. Step back and let them face their own gravity. Your true job is not to be a shield. Believe they are strong enough to stand up. Let them experience the struggle of the dirt. True maturity requires facing difficult trials. If they never fall down, how will they stand?
