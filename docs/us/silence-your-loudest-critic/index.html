Silence Your Loudest Critic

Published 2026-09-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The loudest voice is often yours. You believe every thought represents absolute truth. The human brain generates sixty thousand thoughts. This happens daily according to science. Most repetitive thoughts are ancient survival instincts. Treating criticism as fact creates mental exhaustion. Constant self judgment raises baseline anxiety levels. This effect lingers for weeks at a time. You argue with an internal narrator. It only exists to keep you cautious. When you stop fighting, what voice remains? Can you hear the calm waiting underneath?
