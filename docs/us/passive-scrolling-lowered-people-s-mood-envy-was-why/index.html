Passive Scrolling Lowered People's Mood - Envy Was Why

Published 2026-09-14 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Scrolling isn't what lowers your mood. Comparing is. Researchers texted people five times a day to ask how they felt. The more they scrolled without posting or talking to anyone, the worse they felt later. The reason wasn't time. It was envy: their normal day against everyone's best one. So don't just watch.
