No Problem Is Too Big, Only Too Undivided

Published 2026-09-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You're staring at an overwhelming problem. Feeling completely stuck and truly helpless. You believe the problem is simply insurmountable. Too complex, too large to even begin tackling. The weight of the whole challenge truly paralyzes you. You see the mountain, not the first single step. But no problem is truly too big; it's just undivided. Every massive challenge is a collection of small tasks. The path forward is always a series of micro-actions. Take your biggest problem and write the absolute smallest step. Then take that one single step immediately, no waiting. What challenge is paralyzing you instead of being dissected?
