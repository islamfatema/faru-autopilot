Respect What You Already Own

Published 2026-09-19 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

We live in a culture that constantly seeks new things. New purchases often feel like an instant upgrade. But this cycle of discarding isn't always progress. It often hides a deeper disregard for resources. And a missed opportunity for quiet satisfaction. The UN estimates global waste will increase 70% by 2050. Billions of items still functional end up in landfills. You don't need to add to that statistic. Maintaining what you own builds a unique self-reliance. It fosters gratitude and a deep connection to your life. This care for your gear reflects care for your goals. Do you truly respect what you already possess?
