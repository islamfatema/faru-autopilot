Glass Is Not A Slow-Moving Liquid

Published 2026-09-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe old glass is slowly melting downward. You heard cathedral windows flow over centuries. It sounds like a fact you picked up at school. And it makes complete sense every time you look at one. Except glass is not a slow liquid at all. It is an amorphous solid that stays put. Those medieval panes are thick simply due to bad manufacturing. They installed the heavy edge at the bottom on purpose. Try it yourself: go look at an old mirror from a century ago. The top and bottom are exact same thickness. What else are you repeating just because it sounded good?
