You're Not Lazy, You're Avoiding Clarity

Published 2026-09-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You're not lazy, you're AVOIDING CLARITY. You believe you're just putting things off. But your brain isn't resisting the WORK. It's resisting the CONFUSION of the start. Overwhelm means no clear FIRST STEP. The task feels too big because it IS undefined. Stop punishing yourself for a blurry beginning. Pick ONE thing you're avoiding right now. Write down the absolute SMALLEST physical action. Just the first touch, the first CLICK, the first WORD. Do that one tiny thing. What if your biggest blocks are blurry starts?
