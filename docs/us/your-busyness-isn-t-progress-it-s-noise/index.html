Your Busyness Isn't Progress, It's Noise

Published 2026-09-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You equate constant busyness with progress. You believe every minute must be occupied. But that belief is a subtle trap. Often, the endless churn of 'doing'... ...is just noise, not real movement. Busyness can be a shield from deep work. A distraction from what truly matters. Progress isn't counted by tasks completed. It's by the impact of the essential few. Today, find one task you do to 'be busy'. Eliminate it, or delegate it now. What real work was it hiding from you?
