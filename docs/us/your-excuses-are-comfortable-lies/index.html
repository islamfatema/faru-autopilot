Your Excuses Are Comfortable Lies

Published 2026-08-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably think your excuses are valid reasons. That your circumstances really are holding you back. But the harsh truth is they are comfortable lies. A way to avoid the discomfort of genuine growth. These narratives serve only to keep you stuck. Psychological studies suggest humans often prefer certainty, even negative certainty, over the risk of the unknown. This preference feeds your reliance on excuses daily. Meaning every time you make an excuse, you erode your self-trust and dim your inner power even more. You're actively choosing to negotiate with your weakness. Are you ready to drop the lie and seize power?
