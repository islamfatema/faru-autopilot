Count The Cost Before You Step

Published 2026-09-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You can have anything you want. Success is about just deciding to achieve it. But every major goal carries a hidden cost. You fixate on the reward, not the extensive payment. This oversight often leads to early failure. Insufficient planning leads to project failure in 70% of cases. This includes underestimating the true sacrifice required. Many commitments fail because the hidden costs are too high. You might gain one thing, but lose three others. Unpaid costs accumulate, creating overwhelming debt. This debt isn't just money; it's time, energy, and peace. Is your future vision truly worth its full price?
