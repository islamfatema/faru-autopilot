Your Growth Needs Less Approval, More Discomfort

Published 2026-09-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Your growth needs less APPROVAL, more discomfort. You believe your path needs everyone's blessing. That universal acceptance is the key to validation. But that desire for it is often a comfortable CAGE. Real evolution needs you to choose *your* own path. Even if others don't understand or disapprove. Discomfort isn't a sign you're wrong, it's expansion. Think of one thing you want to say or do. But you're holding back because of someone else. Tell *that specific person* what you need to say. Or make the choice you've been fearing to make. Who are you pleasing at the cost of your evolution?
