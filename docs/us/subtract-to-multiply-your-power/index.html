Subtract To Multiply Your Power

Published 2026-09-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You often believe that more is always inherently better. That adding tasks and goals increases your overall power. But this belief often dilutes your true impact. It spreads your energy thinly across everything. Leading to less focus and diminished true power. The Pareto Principle states 20% effort yields 80% results. Most additions fall into the 80% of low returns. Therefore, more input doesn't mean more output. Subtracting the non-essential frees up valuable energy. This allows you to amplify your truly impactful efforts. Creating deeper purpose and greater real impact. What essential truth hides behind your many distractions?
