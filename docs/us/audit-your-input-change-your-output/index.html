Audit Your Input, Change Your Output

Published 2026-10-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You wonder why your thoughts feel scattered and cluttered. You assume it's simply a lack of willpower. But the root cause might be your daily input. The information you consume shapes your mental landscape. Cheap noise and distractions erode your focus slowly. On average, adults spend over 6 hours on screens daily. This constant stream overloads your cognitive circuits. It leaves little room for clarity or original thought. Your mental energy is a finite, valuable resource. Consuming low-quality input depletes it without purpose. Upgrading your input means upgrading your mental output. What low-quality input will you choose to filter out?
