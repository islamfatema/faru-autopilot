Your Past Isn't A Prediction, It's Just Data

Published 2026-09-14 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You carry your past like a heavy chain. Believing it predicts your future path. You see old failures as present limits. But your past isn't a prophecy. It's just a dataset of information. Every setback, every wrong turn, every Perceived failure is a point on a chart. It shows you what *was*; not what *will be*. This isn't erasing history, but seeing it. You're not bound by it; you're empowered. Recall one past failure. What specific lesson Can you extract? Apply it to a task today. Run a new experiment.
