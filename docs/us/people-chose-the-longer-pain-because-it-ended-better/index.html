People Chose the Longer Pain Because It Ended Better

Published 2026-09-13 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

People chose more pain, because it ended better. Cold water, 60 seconds. Then 90 seconds, the last 30 slightly warmer. Asked which to repeat, most chose the longer one. Memory doesn't add up minutes; it keeps the worst moment and the last. Doctors saw it in 682 colonoscopy patients. So end today on something good. It's the part you'll remember.
