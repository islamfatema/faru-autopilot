Your Cracks Are Not Weakness

Published 2026-09-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You see your past mistakes or setbacks as fundamental flaws. Evidence of what broke, or where you once failed. And you feel compelled to hide these old cracks. But those aren't flaws. They are vital stress points. The very places where you had to bend and adapt. Where your resilience was forged, not shattered. They prove your profound ability to withstand pressure. Look at one old scar, physical or emotional, today. See it not as a weakness, but as a badge of your strength. Feel the quiet power of your past perseverance. What story of strength does your journey reveal?
