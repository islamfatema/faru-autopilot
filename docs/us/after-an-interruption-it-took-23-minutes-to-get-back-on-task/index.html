After an Interruption, It Took 23 Minutes to Get Back on Task

Published 2026-09-13 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

One interruption can cost you 23 minutes. Researchers at UC Irvine followed office workers through their real days. When someone got pulled off a task, it took an average of 23 minutes to get back to it, usually after two other things in between. Interrupted people worked faster, but felt more stressed.
