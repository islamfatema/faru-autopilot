Not Everything Needs To Be Done

Published 2026-09-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe completing every task makes you highly productive. You think a long to do list shows success. But endless busyness is actually a form of laziness. You are hiding behind small tasks to avoid greatness. Doing useless things efficiently will never make you successful. Economist Vilfredo Pareto proved this way back in 1896. His famous principle shows eighty percent of your results come from only twenty percent of your daily efforts. This means most of your daily work is useless. You are wasting precious energy on things that vanish. You must ignore tasks that do not actually matter. Are you actually productive, or just afraid of silence?
