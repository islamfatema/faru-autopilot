Your Failures Are Not Your Verdict

Published 2026-09-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You see past failures as definitive proof. A permanent verdict on your capabilities. You let them define you, predicting your future. But a failure is an event, not a label. It's a piece of data, not your identity. A stepping stone of insight, not a final judgment. A compass recalibrating your direction. Your failures are not your verdict. Today, name one past "failure" you regret. Write down three specific lessons you gained from it. See it as fuel, not a barrier. What other stories have you mislabeled?
