Your Experience Needs No External Witness

Published 2026-09-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You feel your experience isn't real enough. Not valid until someone else affirms it. You explain, justify, seeking outside approval. But your internal world needs no external witness. Your feelings, your journey, are true on their own terms. Their validity isn't dependent on others. Your truth stands strong without explanation. So, today, write down a feeling you hold. Just for yourself. No intention to share. Feel the release of owning your own truth. What else is waiting for *your* own validation?
