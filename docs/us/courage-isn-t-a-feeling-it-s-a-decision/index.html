Courage Isn't A Feeling, It's A Decision

Published 2026-09-24 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Many believe courage is absence of fear. You wait for it to arrive before you take the leap. But that is not how bravery works. Your brain's fear center doesn't just switch off. Courage is a decision to act despite the fear. It's a mental muscle that strengthens with use. You don't conquer fear you move with it. Each small step builds your capacity for more. So, take one small step today toward a fear. It will not disappear but you will grow. What fear are you letting masquerade as a wall?
