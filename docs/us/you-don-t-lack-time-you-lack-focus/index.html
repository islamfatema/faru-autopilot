You Don't Lack Time, You Lack Focus

Published 2026-09-06 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You don't lack time, you lack FOCUS. You tell yourself there aren't enough hours. But everyone gets the exact same 24. The problem isn't the clock's scarcity. It's how your attention is CONSTANTLY fractured. We mistake activity for true productivity. Think of one task today you really need to do. For the next 30 minutes, dedicate yourself ONLY to it. Silence your phone. Close all other tabs. Be entirely PRESENT with that one thing. Notice how much you actually ACCOMPLISH. Where else are you trading presence for busyness?
