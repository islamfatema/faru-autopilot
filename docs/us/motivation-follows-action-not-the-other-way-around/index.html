Motivation Follows Action, Not The Other Way Around

Published 2026-09-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You're waiting for motivation to strike. You believe you need to feel inspired first. That burst of energy before you can start. But that feeling rarely comes when you're static. True motivation isn't a starting fuel. It's a byproduct of momentum itself. It emerges after you actually begin. When you see even a small piece of progress. Stop waiting for the feeling. Take one tiny, meaningless step towards something. Notice how the desire to do more gently emerges. What small step can you take today to unlock momentum?
