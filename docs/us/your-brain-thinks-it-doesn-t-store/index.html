Your Brain Thinks, It Doesn't Store

Published 2026-09-11 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think your brain is a giant hard drive? Meant to store every detail, task, and thought? You’re making your most powerful tool less effective. Your brain is for THINKING, not storage. It excels at processing, connecting, creating ideas. Not at being a perfect archive for endless lists. When you overload it, you slow its true function. Free up its capacity for creative thought. Today, write down three things you’re trying to remember. Move them off your mind and onto paper or a note. Feel the immediate lift and new mental space. What will you THINK when your brain is free?
