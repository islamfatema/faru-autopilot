Choose Your Sacrifice

Published 2026-09-22 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You might believe you can have it all. That success comes from getting more. But true growth demands subtraction. The greatest achievements require deliberate cuts. You can't add without taking away. Your brain makes 35,000 choices daily. That staggering number proves focus is key. You cannot give full attention to all. This means your energy is a finite resource. Without conscious choices, it gets spread too thin. Leading to burnout and wasted potential. What are you willing to consciously give up?
