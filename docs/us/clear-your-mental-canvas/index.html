Clear Your Mental Canvas

Published 2026-09-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You cannot paint on top of a crowded canvas. You must let go of what is finished. Clear out the old projects and regrets. Create a blank space in your mind. In 1956, George Miller discovered a mental limit. Working memory holds only seven items. Your brain cannot process active distractions. Too many open loops consume your capacity. When you stop carrying yesterday's clutter, You finally free up your limited bandwidth. This lets you fully shape today. How many unfinished tasks are stealing focus?
