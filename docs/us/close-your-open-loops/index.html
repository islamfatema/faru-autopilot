Close Your Open Loops

Published 2026-10-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Your exhaustion comes from half-finished work. Unreplied emails and chores drain your energy. Open loops deplete your mental battery continuously. Stop letting tasks pile up around you. The Zeigarnik effect explains this drain. Uncompleted tasks occupy our attention constantly. Your brain spends hidden energy tracking obligations. Unfinished items create constant background stress. Pick one small lingering task today. Finish it completely and restore your focus. Feel the heavy weight lift from your mind. What draining task have you avoided this week?
