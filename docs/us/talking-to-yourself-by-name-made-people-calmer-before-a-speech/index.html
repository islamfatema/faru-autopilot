Talking to Yourself by Name Made People Calmer Before a Speech

Published 2026-09-13 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

When you're nervous, stop saying 'I'. Michigan researchers had people give a speech with almost no time to prepare. Some talked to themselves as 'I'. Others used their own name: why is Sarah nervous? The name group felt less anxious, and judges rated their speeches better. Before your next hard thing, say your name. Talk to yourself like a friend. Write what you'd tell yourself - using your own name. number 7: where your mind goes almost half the time you're awake.

Sources

  • Kross et al. 2014, J. Personality & Social Psychology 106(2) - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24467424/
