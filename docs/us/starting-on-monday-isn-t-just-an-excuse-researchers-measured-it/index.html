What the video says

Starting on Monday isn't just an excuse. Wharton researchers tracked what people do when a new week begins. Searches for 'diet' jump at the start of every week, month and year. Gym visits rise after a birthday. It's the fresh start effect. The old you feels like someone else. So pick your Monday. Then show up on it.
