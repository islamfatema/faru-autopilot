Your Multitasking Isn't Efficiency, It's Dilution

Published 2026-09-06 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe multitasking is peak efficiency. Juggling tasks, tabs, and endless notifications. You feel busy, productive, in the constant flurry. But your attention is truly fragmented. Your vital energy is simply diluted. Each task receives only a fraction. True efficiency is not about more. It's doing the right thing with undivided focus. A single, focused beam cuts deeper than a floodlight. Close tabs, silence alerts, focus on one task. Notice the clarity and quality of your output. What depth is lost in constant distraction?
