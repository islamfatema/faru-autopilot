Excavate Your Own Truth

Published 2026-09-22 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You might believe your core truths are entirely your own making. That you've shaped them through all your experiences. But much of what you hold dear was planted by someone else. Many fundamental views are inherited, not chosen by you. They aren't necessarily your deeply authentic foundation. Psychologists suggest many core beliefs form before age 7. These deeply embedded ideas guide your daily decisions. Often operating entirely below your conscious thought. This means you might be building your life on sand. You risk living out a script that isn't truly yours. Authenticity demands this crucial inner excavation for growth. Is your foundation solid, or just comfortably familiar?
