Students' Worst-Case Deadline Guesses Were Still Too Short

Published 2026-09-14 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Even your worst-case guess is probably too optimistic. Psychologists asked students when they'd finish their thesis. Average guess: 34 days. If everything went wrong: 49. Actual: 55 and a half. Their worst case wasn't bad enough. It's called the planning fallacy. You picture the plan, not the interruptions. So take your honest guess, then plan past your worst one.
