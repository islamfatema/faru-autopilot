Forgetting Is Your Brain Cleaning House

Published 2026-10-06 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably believe forgetting details is a flaw. A sign your mind is losing its sharp edge. But this common idea is actually mistaken. Your brain isn't decaying, it's actively optimizing. It's doing something powerful and intentional. Neuroscience research, detailed in the journal Science, shows Synaptic pruning, studied since the 1980s, confirms this. Your brain deliberately clears space by shedding old data. This frees up vital cognitive resources for you. It enhances your capacity for creative problem-solving. Meaning you have more focus for new challenges. What fresh insights are you missing by clinging?
