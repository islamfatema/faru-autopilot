Ancient Romans Drank Cold Snow Shipped Across Mountains

Published 2026-10-22 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think refrigerators invented cold drinks. You believe ancient people drank everything lukewarm. But wealthy Romans paid couriers a fortune to haul winter snow down from the Apennine peaks. They packed ice in pits lined with thick straw to chill wine and fruit during August heatwaves. Open your freezer door and grab an ice cube. You take instant ice for granted every day. Romans used mountain mules for the exact same luxury. How much would you pay for ice hauled by hand?
