People Made Toothpaste 1,600 Years Ago - Here's the Recipe

Published 2026-09-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

People were making toothpaste 1,600 years ago. A papyrus from Roman Egypt, written in the 4th century, gives the recipe for a powder for white and perfect teeth. Rock salt, mint, dried iris flower and grains of pepper, crushed together. A dentist who tried it in 2003 said it made his gums bleed - but left his mouth fresh and clean.
