Why Do We Still Teach Freud

Published 2026-11-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Modern psychology did not discard Freud entirely. That academic belief is completely wrong. Universities still teach him because of talk therapy. Before 1900, patients faced brutal asylum confinement. Freud proved verbalizing trauma relieves nervous disorders. Try talking through a heavy stressful problem today. Notice how simple conversation reduces your mental pressure. Why do we still rely on conversation to heal minds?
