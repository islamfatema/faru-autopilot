Ancient Romans Never Used Pockets in Their Togas

Published 2026-10-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume historical clothing had hidden pockets. Most picture garments equipped to store coins. The Roman toga had zero pockets or fasteners. It was a massive wool sheet twenty feet long. Gravity and complex folds kept the robe in place. Reach into your pocket and pull out your keys now. Feel how much storage you carry around daily. Imagine navigating a city holding things in folds. Roman citizens walked carefully to avoid drops. How long before you lost your keys in public?
