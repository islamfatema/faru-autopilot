Why Chef Hats Have Exactly 100 Folds ‍

Published 2026-10-26 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

A chef's hat is just a uniform. Its pleats are mostly for decoration. But the number of pleats matters deeply. It signifies much more than just style. Around 1820, master chef Carême set the standard. He decreed 100 folds showed 100 ways to cook an egg. This tradition elevates the craft and culinary mastery. It transforms a simple meal into an art form. Does knowing this history make your meal taste better? Or change how you view your chef?
