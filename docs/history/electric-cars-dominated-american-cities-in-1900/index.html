Electric Cars Dominated American Cities in 1900

Published 2026-11-02 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe electric cars are a modern invention. That common assumption is completely wrong. Gas cars were actually the minority option. Drivers in 1900 hated gasoline noise and smoke. Electric taxicabs dominated New York City streets. In 1900, 38 percent of US cars were electric. Gas engines held only 22 percent of the market. Ford's cheap gas assembly killed the battery market. Try it yourself: check your 12-volt battery. Its lead-acid chemical system was designed in 1859. Did cheap gasoline set battery tech back a century?
