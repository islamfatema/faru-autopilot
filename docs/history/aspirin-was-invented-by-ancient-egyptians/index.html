Aspirin Was Invented by Ancient Egyptians

Published 2026-09-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think modern painkillers are a recent invention? Something created by pharmaceutical science? Then you might be surprised by what ancient cultures knew. The active ingredient in Aspirin is salicylic acid. And ancient Egyptians were using it. They used willow bark, which is rich in it, to relieve pain and fever THOUSANDS of years ago. Greeks like Hippocrates also prescribed it. The 'modern' drug simply refined this ancient knowledge. Next time you take a painkiller, consider its ancient roots. What other wisdom have we forgotten?
