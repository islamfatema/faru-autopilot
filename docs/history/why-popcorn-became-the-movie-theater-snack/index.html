Why Popcorn Became the Movie Theater Snack

Published 2026-10-18 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably can't imagine movies without popcorn. It's the quintessential cinema snack, always has been. But early movie theaters actually banned snacks inside. Popcorn was considered too messy and rather low-class. Theater owners wanted high-end patrons, not sticky floors. Then the Great Depression changed everything in the 1930s. Desperate for any revenue, they allowed cheap street vendors. Popcorn machines were soon brought into theater lobbies. This decision saved many cinemas from financial ruin. It created the iconic movie and popcorn pairing. Your modern cinema experience was shaped by this change. Is popcorn still an essential part of your movie?
