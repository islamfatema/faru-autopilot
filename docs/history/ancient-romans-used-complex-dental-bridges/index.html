Ancient Romans Used Complex Dental Bridges

Published 2026-10-13 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume advanced dentistry is purely modern science. You picture ancient people living with missing teeth. That medical belief is entirely incorrect. Roman dentists built gold wire bridges in 100 BCE. Practitioners secured bone teeth into empty jaw gaps. Gold bands held replacements firmly against chewing pressure. Try looking closely at a modern wire retainer. It uses the exact tension applied by Roman surgeons. Wealthy citizens bought smiles crafted from precious metal.
