Why the Alphabet Order Is ABC and Not Random

Published 2026-09-10 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Most people assume the alphabet order is random. That belief is entirely wrong. The ABC order is over 3,000 years old. Phoenicians arranged it around 1050 BC. Greeks copied it for their own language. Then Romans adapted it for Latin writing. Because Rome won their sequence survived. Your phone keyboard uses this ancient layout. Every modern text message relies on 3000 year old choices. Ancient trade routes still dictate your typing today. We are locked into old Mediterranean standards. Why did they never change it?
