Why Cars Still Have a Glove Compartment

Published 2026-10-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Most drivers think the glove box is for registration papers and insurance cards. In 1915, early automobiles completely lacked roofs, windshields, and cabin heaters. Motorists wore thick leather gloves for warmth against bitter cold and road debris. Carmakers built small dash compartments specifically to store those driving gloves. Modern cars kept the traditional storage name long after handwear disappeared from transit. This leaves valuable dashboard space claimed by outdated terminology.
