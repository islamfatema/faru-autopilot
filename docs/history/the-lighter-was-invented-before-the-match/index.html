The Lighter Was Invented Before the Match

Published 2026-09-20 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The lighter was invented before the match. In 1823, a German chemist named Johann Döbereiner built a lamp that lit itself. Hydrogen gas flowed over platinum, and the platinum made it catch fire. Thousands were sold. The friction match - strike it, and it lights - came from an English pharmacist, John Walker, three years later. We had the lighter first. The match just took over.
