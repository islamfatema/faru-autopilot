Roman Sandals Were Better Than Modern Army Boots

Published 2026-10-29 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume Roman sandals were cheap, weak footwear. You think modern boots are far superior. That assumption is completely and dangerously wrong. Modern armies lose thousands to wet foot rot. In 15 AD, legionaries marched 20 miles daily, wearing open leather sandals studded with 80 iron nails. The open-air design kept their feet bone dry, stopping fungal rot before it could ruin armies. The greatest empire was built on open ventilation. Try blowing air through a damp sock right now. Notice how trapped water stays wet and cold. Did modern military gear actually downgrade us?
