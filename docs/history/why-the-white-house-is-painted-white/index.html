Why the White House Is Painted White

Published 2026-10-19 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Most Americans believe it was designed to be white. That patriotic theory is completely wrong. Construction finished in 1792 with gray sandstone. Porous stone absorbed water during winter freezes. In 1798, workers applied a lime-based whitewash. The coating protected stone from cracking in ice. That functional maintenance created an icon. The white color was never an aesthetic choice. Preservation saved the walls from weather damage. Your national symbol exists thanks to masonry sealer. Utility shaped design more than politics. Should preservation override original architecture?
