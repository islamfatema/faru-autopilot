Why We Clap Our Hands to Show Approval

Published 2026-10-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Everyone thinks clapping is a natural impulse. Modern audiences believe it always existed. Ancient Roman theater audiences used different methods. Historical texts from one hundred AD note this. Romans snapped fingers and waved toga ends. Medieval European theaters later standardized hand clapping. Large crowds needed a unified sound. Your modern concert applause stems from regulations. This changes how you express social approval. Every time you clap, you follow Roman rules. Are you blindly copying ancient theater crowds? Why not invent a better noise?
