Why We Change the Clocks Twice a Year

Published 2026-10-14 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You likely believe Daylight Saving Time saves energy. Perhaps you think it was designed for farmers. These widespread ideas about DST are mistaken. But Daylight Saving Time does not save energy. And it actually disrupts modern agriculture. This twice-a-year ritual began in World War I. Germany first adopted it on April 30, 1916. Their goal was to conserve coal and resources. Other nations like the US quickly followed suit. This means we still live by a WWI rule. It disrupts our sleep circles and daily routine. Some studies link it to yearly health issues. Should we continue this anachronistic twice-a-year change?
