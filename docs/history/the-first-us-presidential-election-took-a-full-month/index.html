The First US Presidential Election Took a Full Month

Published 2026-11-04 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume the first US election lasted one day. That belief is completely wrong and anachronistic. The historic 1789 vote spanned a full five weeks starting in mid-December across the young nation. Congress allowed a wide calendar window because horseback messengers took weeks crossing counties. Citizens voted on varied dates as weather allowed travel along icy roads. Time how fast a package arrives across your state to see how slow travel once shaped nations. Why do we still vote on a single modern Tuesday?
