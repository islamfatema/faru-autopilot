Ancient Egyptians Did Not Speak Hieroglyphics Daily

Published 2026-10-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You imagine Egyptians writing hieroglyphs daily. That classroom myth ignores how complex it was. Hieroglyphs were sacred carvings for temple walls. Painting one bird symbol took hours of precise work. Citizens used a fast script called hieratic ink. Everyday writing looked nothing like stone carvings. Pick up a pen and write your name as fast as possible. Feel how cursive letters flow together to save time. Ancient scribes abandoned pictures for rapid ink. When did your handwriting last change completely?
