Why Theater Stages Have Upstage and Downstage

Published 2026-11-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Why do actors say 'upstage' and 'downstage'? In the Renaissance, stages were built on a physical incline. This sloped design helped the audience see performers in the back. Moving away from the crowd meant walking uphill, or 'upstage'. Moving toward the crowd was walking downhill, or 'downstage'. The physical slopes are gone, but the terms survived.
