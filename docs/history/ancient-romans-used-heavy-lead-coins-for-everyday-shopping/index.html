Ancient Romans Used Heavy Lead Coins for Everyday Shopping

Published 2026-10-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume ancient currency was strictly gold and silver. That belief is completely wrong for daily markets. Ordinary shoppers relied on cheap lead tokens. In 70 AD, urban shops used lead for small purchases. Precious metal coins were too valuable for daily bread. Millions of heavy lead weights survived in ruins. Weigh a metal coin in your hand right now. Feel how physical weight defined ancient trade. Small transactions required discardable metal tokens. Everyday commerce ignored shiny imperial gold.
