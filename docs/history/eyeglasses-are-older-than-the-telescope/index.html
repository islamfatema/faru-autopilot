Eyeglasses Are Older Than The Telescope

Published 2026-10-24 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You might think telescopes are older... ...than eyeglasses. Most people assume seeing far came first. But seeing *clearly* was invented centuries earlier. The first eyeglasses appeared in Italy around 1286, making reading and fine work possible. The telescope, for viewing distant stars, wasn't publicly shown until 1608. To see how a lens works, hold a glass of water over a word. It magnifies and distorts it. What other everyday tech feels more modern?
