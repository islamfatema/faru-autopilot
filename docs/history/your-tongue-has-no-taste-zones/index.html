Your Tongue Has No Taste Zones

Published 2026-09-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Your tongue does not have different zones. You were taught that sweet is on the tip. And you probably drew that map in school. But millions of textbooks are completely wrong. The myth started in 1942 from a bad translation of a German research paper written back in 1901. Every single taste bud can detect every flavor. There are no boundaries anywhere on your tongue. Try it yourself: put salt on the tip. You will taste it instantly, proving the map is fake. Why do schools still teach this false map?
