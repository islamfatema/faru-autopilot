Why Cars Have an Emergency or "Hand" Brake

Published 2026-10-31 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You know your car's handbrake is for emergencies. A backup in case the main brakes fail. But it wasn't always a secondary safety feature. It was once the primary way to stop a car. Early automobiles relied on these manual levers. Foot-operated brakes evolved to be more powerful. This evolution means your car is much safer now. With two distinct systems for stopping and parking. Should modern cars still include a manual handbrake? Or is electric parking the only future we need?
