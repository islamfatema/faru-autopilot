The Oldest Known Customer Complaint Is Over 3,700 Years Old

Published 2026-09-14 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The oldest known customer complaint is written in clay. Ur, around 1750 BC. Nanni paid a merchant, Ea-nasir, for copper. The copper was bad, and his servant was sent away empty-handed. So Nanni pressed it into clay: who did Ea-nasir think he was, treating him with such contempt? It's in the British Museum. Ea-nasir is still famous for bad service.
