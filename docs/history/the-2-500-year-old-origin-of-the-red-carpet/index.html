The 2,500-Year-Old Origin of the Red Carpet

Published 2026-10-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Red carpets mean fame, luxury, and Hollywood, right? But its origin is ancient and quite unexpected. It first appeared as a symbol of impending tragedy. Its first mention is in Greece, dated 458 BC. In the play Agamemnon, a crimson path was laid. It was for a king, but red was only for gods. Walking it risked divine wrath, not celebrity status. It was a dangerous choice with dire implications. Today, we celebrate on a path once deeply feared. Are we ignoring the warning from 2,500 years ago?
