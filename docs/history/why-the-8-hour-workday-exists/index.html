Why the 8-Hour Workday Exists

Published 2026-10-26 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The 8-hour workday feels like an ancient rule. We assume this daily structure is simply normal. But it was popularized less than a century ago. Early 1900s factory shifts were 10 to 12 hours. That changed in September 1926 at Ford Motor Company. Henry Ford instituted the 40-hour workweek. He found giving workers more rest boosted productivity. This innovation actually fueled the consumer economy. It means our modern concept of leisure changed. Companies discovered happy workers buy more products. This created the very idea of a weekend culture. Forever shifting the balance of work and life. Is our current work schedule truly optimal today?
