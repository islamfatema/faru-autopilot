The Rolling Stones Are Older Than the Computer Mouse

Published 2026-10-30 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume computer mice predate modern rock bands. You think digital interfaces came before stadium tours. The Rolling Stones played their first show in 1962. A computer mouse did not exist anywhere yet. Douglas Engelbart built the first prototype in 1968. That is a six-year gap before the first cursor moved. Mick Jagger was already famous before desktop screens. Place your hand on your computer mouse right now. Slide it across your desk one slow inch. How many decades of music happened before this tool?
