Ancient Romans Used Urine to Wash Clothes

Published 2026-10-17 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You likely think laundry soaps are modern inventions. You are completely wrong about ancient cleaning. Roman fullers gathered massive amounts of urine. Stale human waste breaks down into ammonia. This natural chemical stripped grease from wool. Laundromats paid an imperial tax on the fluid. Citizens left jars on busy street corners. Commercial washers relied on public collections. Try smelling household ammonia to test this. Ancient street sanitation powered the clothing trade. How much would you pay a cleaner using this method?
