Why Forks Have Exactly Four Tines

Published 2026-11-06 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

For centuries, forks had two or three tines. Hard to scoop grains or hold food. Late 1700s: King Ferdinand of Naples has a problem. He wants an easy way to eat spaghetti. His chamberlain designs a brand new fork. Adding a fourth tine to grip pasta. Creating the standard fork we use today.
