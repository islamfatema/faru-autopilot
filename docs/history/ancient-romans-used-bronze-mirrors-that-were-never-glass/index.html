Ancient Romans Used Bronze Mirrors That Were Never Glass

Published 2026-10-02 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Most people assume mirrors have always been made of glass. That historical assumption is completely wrong. Roman craftsmen forged them from solid bronze. Reflective metal discs replaced glass completely. Every reflection had a strange golden or grey tint. Noblewomen had to polish their mirrors with pumice. Hold a shiny metal spoon up to your face right now. Your kitchen silverware works like a Roman mirror. Imagine brushing your hair using a cloudy bronze disc. How clear is your reflection in a polished metal pan?
