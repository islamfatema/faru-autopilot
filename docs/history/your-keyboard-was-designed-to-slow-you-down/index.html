Your Keyboard Was Designed to Slow You Down

Published 2026-10-24 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Your keyboard was designed to slow you down. You assume the layout is optimized for speed. But the QWERTY design has a hidden purpose. Early typewriter arms jammed easily. Typing too quickly broke the machine. So the inventor separated common letter pairs. It was patented in Wisconsin in 1873. Try it yourself: type 'typewriter' now. It only uses the very top row of keys. Why do we still use this broken system?
