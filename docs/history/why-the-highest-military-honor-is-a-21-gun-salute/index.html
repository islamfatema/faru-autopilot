Why the Highest Military Honor is a 21-Gun Salute

Published 2026-11-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The 21-gun salute is the ultimate honor. Its roots are in 17th-century naval law. British warships carried exactly seven cannons. Firing them proved the ship was unarmed. Forts on land had more gunpowder. They fired three shots for every ship shot. Seven times three equals twenty-one. This naval math became law in 1875.
