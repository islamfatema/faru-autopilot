Ancient Romans Used Glowing Rocks as Nightlights

Published 2026-10-08 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think glowing nightlights are a modern invention. You assume ancient Romans slept in total darkness. Wealthy villas used stones that glowed after sundown. Natural fluorite crystals absorbed bright daylight. They emitted an eerie blue light all night. No oil lamps or wicks were needed for this. Walk into a dark room and turn your phone light on. Cover the lens halfway with your thumb. See how the glow spills across your fingertips? Ancient Romans had that same blue light at rest. What else did they build without modern power?
