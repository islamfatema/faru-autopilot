Why We Say "Hello" When Answering the Phone

Published 2026-10-02 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Everyone says hello when answering the phone. You probably think it is an ancient word. That linguistic belief is incorrect. In 1876, the telephone was newly invented. Alexander Graham Bell wanted people to say ahoy. Thomas Edison proposed the word hello instead. Early phone operators popularized Edison choice. These female operators were called hello girls. Their daily habit shaped modern communication. Billions of people repeat Edison word daily. Language changes based on new technology use. Would you answer calls by shouting ahoy today?
