Ancient Romans Kept Live Fish in Dining Room Pools

Published 2026-10-10 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think indoor fish tanks are a modern invention. Wealthy Romans built water channels through dining rooms. Guests sat around marble pools watching dinner swim by. Slaves diverted aqueduct water beneath the banquet tables to ensure the fresh catch stayed cold and active. The chef scooped the fish out right before cooking. You pick up sealed fillets from the grocery aisle. Try picturing your dinner swimming under your chair. How much would you pay for seafood that fresh? Why did we stop bringing our food to the table alive?
