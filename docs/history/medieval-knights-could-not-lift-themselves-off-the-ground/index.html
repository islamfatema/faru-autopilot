Medieval Knights Could Not Lift Themselves Off the Ground

Published 2026-10-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Knights needed cranes to mount their horses. That is what every movie taught you to believe. Every word of it is completely false. Actual battlefield armor weighed just fifty pounds. The weight was distributed across the entire body. Trained soldiers could run and even do cartwheels. Museums displayed heavy nineteenth-century replicas. Those parade displays created a total myth. Try putting a fifty-pound pack snug against your torso. Notice how natural your balance stays. Why do movies keep promoting total fictions?
