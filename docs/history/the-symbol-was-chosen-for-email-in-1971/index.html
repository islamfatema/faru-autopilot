The @ Symbol Was Chosen For Email in 1971

Published 2026-10-06 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The familiar @ symbol was invented for email. It was created purely for computer addressing. But this common belief is actually incorrect. The @ symbol already had a long history. It was never invented just for digital use. Ray Tomlinson chose it for email in 1971. He found it in 16th century accounting texts. It meant 'at the rate of' or 'each' then. This ancient symbol still connects billions globally. Your email address links you through deep history. From ancient ledgers, to your modern inbox. Does its deep history add meaning to your email?
