Why Legal Pads Are Yellow

Published 2026-10-16 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You might think legal pads are yellow to make them stand out or hide dirt. But their iconic color has a practical purpose. It wasn't initially about hiding scraps, nor just making them more visible. In 1888, Thomas Holley first bound paper scraps. By 1900, his pads were dyed a distinct yellow. This specific yellow reduced eye strain for readers. This design means lawyers could read notes easier, especially under the harsh office lighting of the era. Improving readability prevented fatigue during long hours.
