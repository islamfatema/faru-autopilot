Why Hotels Put Chocolates on Your Pillow

Published 2026-11-01 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Many guests assume pillow chocolates are ancient. The reality is this custom started in 1959. Actor Cary Grant stayed at the Mayfair Hotel. He secretly arranged chocolates on a pillow to woo someone. The hotel manager noticed and adopted it for every room. This sweet marketing trick now appears across the globe. It creates an instant sense of high-end luxury for guests. This luxury experience actually costs hotels pennies today. You feel pampered, but the hotel builds it into rates. How much extra would you pay for daily pillow candy?
