Ancient Romans Used Lead Seals to Guard Cargo

Published 2026-10-11 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume security seals are a modern invention. That assumption is wrong, as trade routes proved. Merchants guarded cargo using stamped lead discs. Inspectors pressed seals into soft molten metal. Cracked lead tokens meant goods had been stolen. Archaeologists unearth millions of these seals. Try pressing your thumb into a lump of clay. Look at how easily your print remains fixed. It is the exact same authentication method. Romans tracked global trade with stamped lead tokens. What seals protect your packages today?
