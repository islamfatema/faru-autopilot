The Origin of "Sleep Tight, Don't Let the Bedbugs Bite"

Published 2026-09-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Old beds had ropes for support. Ropes sagged over time and use. Needed tightening with a tool. To "sleep tight." Bedbugs were a real problem then. Especially in straw filled mattresses. So, don't let them bite. A real warning.
