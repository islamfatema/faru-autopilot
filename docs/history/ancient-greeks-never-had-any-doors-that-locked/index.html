Ancient Greeks Never Had Any Doors That Locked

Published 2026-10-13 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume ancient homes had heavy iron locks. You picture paranoid citizens bolting doors at night. That belief is completely wrong for classical Greece. Most residential doorways had no locks whatsoever. Athenian families simply hung woven cloth curtains. They blocked dust and sun instead of stopping thieves. Society relied entirely on neighborhood watchfulness. Social shame kept streets safer than any deadbolt. Try leaving your front door wide open all day long. Who do you trust more: a steel lock or neighbors? What stopped crime before heavy iron keys existed?
