The Fax Machine Is Older Than the Telephone

Published 2026-09-20 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The fax machine is older than the telephone. In 1843, a Scottish clockmaker named Alexander Bain patented a way to send images down a telegraph wire. Two synchronised pendulums scanned a picture line by line at each end. Alexander Graham Bell's telephone patent came thirty-three years later, in 1876.
