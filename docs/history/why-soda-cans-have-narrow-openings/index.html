Why Soda Cans Have Narrow Openings

Published 2026-11-01 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You might think soda cans have small openings for grip. Perhaps it's for easier pouring or just good modern looks. However, the real reason is far more about hard cash. Its design was not for your convenience or enjoyment. In the 1960s, when pull-tabs became standard on cans, narrow tops saved manufacturers millions of dollars in aluminum. This cost-cutting measure kept your soda more affordable. It directly impacted the price you pay for every drink. Consider convenience versus the savings from these small tops. Would you pay more for a wider can opening today?
