Why Scissors Blades Cross Over

Published 2026-10-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You likely assume cutting tools always had crossed blades. That common belief is entirely wrong. Early cutting tools were U-shaped bronze shears. Ancient Romans and Egyptians used them in 1000 BCE. Two blades were connected by a flexible spring. Inventors did not cross blades until around 100 CE. They placed two blades over a central pin. This provided much greater cutting leverage. The mechanical pivot revolutionized ancient surgery. Tailors across the empire abandoned spring shears. Your office scissors use this Roman pin. How many items rely on this pivot?
