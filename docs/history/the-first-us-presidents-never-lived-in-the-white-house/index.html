The First US Presidents Never Lived in the White House

Published 2026-11-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume Washington lived inside the White House. You picture every leader in that famous mansion. That presidential assumption is completely false. The White House was built and finished in eighteen hundred. Washington served his terms in New York and Philly. John Adams was the first to sleep in the building. Early American leaders worked out of rented rooms. Try packing your office into a half built town. The executive branch once moved like nomads. What famous landmark has a surprisingly short history?
