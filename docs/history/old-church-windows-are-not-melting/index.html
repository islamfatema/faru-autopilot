Old Church Windows Are Not Melting

Published 2026-09-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Ancient cathedral windows are not melting. You were taught that glass is a slow liquid. And that gravity drags it down over centuries. Even some physics teachers repeat this famous lie. But flowing glass would take trillions of years. The uneven thickness is just from old methods. Medieval glassblowers spun discs that had thick edges. Builders placed the heavy ends down for stability. Try it yourself: find an old window pane. Feel the ripples made by the maker's hands. Why do we love to repeat scientific myths?
