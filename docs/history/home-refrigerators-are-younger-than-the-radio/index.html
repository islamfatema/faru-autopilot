Home Refrigerators Are Younger Than The Radio

Published 2026-10-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think refrigerators were always for home? Think again. Their story is industrial. The first mechanical fridge appeared in 1834. It was for breweries and meatpackers. Not for your milk or leftovers. Home refrigerators only got common... In the 1920s, after the radio was invented. Your grandparents heard music... Before they had a modern icebox. Imagine keeping food cool without one... You'd see why early fridges were so vital.
