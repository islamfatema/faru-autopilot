Ancient Greeks Used Lead Weights to Cheat at Elections

Published 2026-10-17 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume ancient voting was entirely honest and safe. Athenian politicians rigged ballots as early as 400 BC. They stuffed bronze tokens with heavy lead cores. Magistrates used balance scales to weigh every single vote. A heavy token meant someone tried to buy the election. Guards arrested ballot stuffers on the assembly steps. Pick up a heavy coin in your pocket right now. Feel that dense metal weight sitting in your palm. It is the exact weight of an ancient rigged vote. Democracy had to invent scales before it invented trust. What election safeguard today will fail future voters?
