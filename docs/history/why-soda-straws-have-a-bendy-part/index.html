Why Soda Straws Have a Bendy Part

Published 2026-10-18 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably think flexible straws were always standard. In 1937, Joseph Friedman invented the bendy design. He wrapped dental floss around a paper tube over a metal screw to shape it. This created permanent ridges that allowed easy bending. Every hospital patient now uses flexible drinking tubes. Millions of rigid straws were discarded worldwide. Beverage companies quickly adopted this new patent. Today every hospital patient uses flexible tubes. You never have to tilt cups at awkward angles. Liquid flows smoothly while you lie flat. Does this simple 1937 patent beat modern plastic? Are paper versions actually better today?
