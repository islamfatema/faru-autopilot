Why US Highway Numbers Aren't Random

Published 2026-09-30 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably think highways are numbered completely at random. In 1956, America designed a strict national grid. To help drivers navigate without modern maps, north-south roads received strictly odd numbers. East-west roads were given predictable even numbers. Route numbers increase from west to east. They also increase from south to north. This system prevents drivers from getting lost. Every interstate sign reveals your exact location. Federal infrastructure planning created this hidden map. Drivers use this logic without realizing it. Could you cross America using only road numbers?
