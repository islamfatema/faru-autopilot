Samurai Existed When Fax Machines Were Invented

Published 2026-11-10 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Samurai lived at the same time as fax machines. You think those warriors vanished in the dark ages. You picture them long before any modern office tech. Yet in 1876, the very year telephones were patented, and early fax systems were tested in offices, traditional samurai armies were still fighting battles. Try writing a quick note and sliding it under a door. Imagine sending that paper across the globe instantly, while a warrior with a sword watches it arrive. How would you bridge those two distant worlds?
