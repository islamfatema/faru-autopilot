Why Standard Wine Bottles Are 750ml

Published 2026-10-01 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Wine bottles hold 750 milliliters today. Many assume scientists chose this exact size. That idea is entirely incorrect. In the 1800s, bottles were hand blown. Glassblowers shaped every single bottle. Human lung capacity was strictly limited. Blowers could only push so much air out. That physical limit averaged 750 milliliters. This biological constraint shaped global trade. You are drinking wine shaped by human lungs. Does this physical limit surprise you today? What other old limits still shape our world?
