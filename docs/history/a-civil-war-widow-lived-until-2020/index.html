A Civil War Widow Lived Until 2020

Published 2026-11-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

We view the Civil War as ancient history. But the last widow of a veteran died in 2020. In 1936, 17-year-old Helen Viola Jackson married a 93-year-old Union soldier. She kept the marriage secret for decades. She lived to see the era of smartphones.
