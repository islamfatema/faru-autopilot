Why Roman Soldiers Wore Socks With Sandals

Published 2026-10-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think wearing socks with sandals is a crime. But the toughest army in history did it daily. Roman soldiers marching across freezing Europe wore thick woolen socks inside leather sandals. They did not care about modern fashion. In nineteen seventy-three, archeologists in England found letters from soldiers begging for more socks. They called them udones, and they saved lives. Try it yourself: walk on cold gravel barefoot. Now put on thick wool. Sandals protect the sole. The sock keeps you warm. It is the ultimate design. Would you wear them if your life depended on it?
