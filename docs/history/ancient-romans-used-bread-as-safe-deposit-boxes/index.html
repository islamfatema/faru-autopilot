Ancient Romans Used Bread as Safe Deposit Boxes

Published 2026-10-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think ancient wealth was kept in leather pouches. That belief ignores how Roman merchants hid gold. They hollowed out raw dough and dropped coins inside. Then they baked the loaf with an official clay seal. Thieves slicing bread could break a knife on gold. Dinner rolls doubled as high-security vaults. Try pressing a heavy coin into a ball of raw dough. Bake it and see how easily metal hides inside starch. Every loaf in the market could be a hidden fortune. This edible security system outsmarted every thief. What everyday item would you hide valuables in?
