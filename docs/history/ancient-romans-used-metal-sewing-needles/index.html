Ancient Romans Used Metal Sewing Needles

Published 2026-10-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume ancient clothing was crudely stitched together. That assumption is completely incorrect. Roman tailors used fine bronze and iron sewing needles. These metal tools featured surprisingly tiny eyelets. Craftspeople stitched heavy wool and leather with precision. Pompeii ruins are packed with these miniature needles. Try threading a sewing needle in dim room light. Ancient manufacturing was sharper than most people think. Your clothes are repaired using two-thousand-year-old tech. Could you survive as a tailor in ancient Rome?
