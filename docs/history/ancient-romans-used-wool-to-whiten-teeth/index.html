Ancient Romans Used Wool to Whiten Teeth

Published 2026-10-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think ancient dental care was entirely primitive. You picture people simply living with stained teeth. Roman writers recorded a fierce obsession with whiteness. They brushed with crushed bones and sharp oyster shells. And the active ingredient was shockingly intense. Romans imported urine from distant provinces. Portuguese urine was prized for its high ammonia content. It acted as a powerful bleaching agent for enamel. Go look at your toothpaste label and check the ingredients. Would you brush your teeth with imported ammonia?
