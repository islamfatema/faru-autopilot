The Secret Service Was Not Made to Protect Presidents

Published 2026-09-02 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The Secret Service was not made for presidents. On April 14, 1865, Lincoln signed the law. Its goal: stop counterfeit money. One-third of US cash was fake at the time. Lincoln was shot that very night. Protection duties only began in 1901.
