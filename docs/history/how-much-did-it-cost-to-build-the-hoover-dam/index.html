How Much Did It Cost to Build the Hoover Dam

Published 2026-11-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The Hoover Dam cost a fortune to build. Completed in 1936, it totaled forty-nine million. That is just one billion in today's money. Less than a single modern football stadium. Over one hundred men died pouring the concrete. The government got every penny back through power sales. Calculate your city's power bill for one single month. What mega-project could we fund today?
