Ancient Rome Had Multi-Story Shopping Malls

Published 2026-10-04 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume indoor shopping malls are a modern invention. Trajan's Market in Rome had shops on multiple levels. Built in 110 AD, it featured over 150 independent stores. Vaulted concrete arcades kept customers out of the rain. Merchants sold silk, spices, and wine in covered halls. Shoppers walked down stone corridors just like today. The design maximized retail space in a dense city. Try walking through a modern mall and checking the layout. Ancient Romans invented this exact commercial structure.
