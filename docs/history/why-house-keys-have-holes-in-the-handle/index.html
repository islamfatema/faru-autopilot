Why House Keys Have Holes in the Handle

Published 2026-09-11 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Most people believe key holes are for keychains. That belief is completely wrong. Ancient Roman keys had large rings. Citizens wore them on their fingers. It stopped theft in crowded cities. Makers kept the hole for modern rings. That tiny opening has a 2000 year history. Your daily pocket items reflect ancient Roman crime. Security design has barely evolved. You carry your keys just like a Roman citizen. Old anti theft methods created modern hardware. What else are we wearing out of habit?
