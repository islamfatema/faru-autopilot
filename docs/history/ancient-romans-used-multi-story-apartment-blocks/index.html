Ancient Romans Used Multi-Story Apartment Blocks

Published 2026-10-27 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume high-rise apartment blocks are modern inventions. Urban density born from twentieth-century cities. You were taught ancient peoples lived in one-offs. Romans built six-story brick tenements called insulae. Thousands crammed into dense apartment blocks in 100 AD. Buildings had multiple floors and shared stairwells. Emperors set height limits to stop structural collapses. Try looking up at a modern city apartment building today. Notice the vertical stacking used by Roman landlords. You are looking at ancient urban housing design. What other modern city crises started in Rome?
