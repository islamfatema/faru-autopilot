Ancient Romans Wore Purple Dyed Robes Worth More Than Gold

Published 2026-10-20 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably picture emperors wearing simple white togas. That picture is entirely wrong for top Roman elites. Imperial purple dye was worth more than solid gold. It came from millions of tiny sea snails in the sea. Two hundred fifty thousand mollusks made one pound. Unauthorised purple clothing was a crime ending in death. Senators walked around wearing portable bank vaults. Check the current price of gold per ounce. Imagine paying that price for just one new shirt. Status was worn directly on your back in public. Would you spend that fortune on your daily outfit? What luxury items today will seem crazy tomorrow?
