The Color Orange Is Younger Than The Fruit

Published 2026-10-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Or autumn leaves, without the word 'orange'. It wasn't until the 16th century... When 'oranges' spread from Asia... That the color got its own name. Try describing a pumpkin without... Using the word 'orange'. How hard is it?
