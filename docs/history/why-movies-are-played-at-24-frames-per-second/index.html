Why Movies Are Played at 24 Frames Per Second

Published 2026-11-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Silent movies ran at variable speeds. But in 1926, synchronized sound changed everything. Audio was printed directly onto the film strip. It required a constant, steady speed to sound natural. Studios agreed on 24 frames per second. It was the cheapest speed for clear sound. It remains the cinema standard today.
