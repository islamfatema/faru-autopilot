Ancient Romans Had Professional Hairdressers

Published 2026-10-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think complex hairstyles need modern hair salons. That common belief is entirely mistaken. Wealthy Roman women hired specialized enslaved hairdressers. Ornatrices built towering curls every single morning. They used heated iron rods called calamistra for styling. Wire frames held massive braids in place all day. Try holding a hot iron for ten straight minutes. Your morning routine is nothing compared to ancient Rome. Status was worn right on top of your head. Who spends more time on hair you or a Roman matron?
