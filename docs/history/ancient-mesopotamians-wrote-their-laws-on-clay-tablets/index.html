Ancient Mesopotamians Wrote Their Laws on Clay Tablets

Published 2026-10-17 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume ancient laws were carved into marble. You picture deep chisel marks on royal stone. Early legal codes started on soft wet river mud. Scribes pressed cuneiform wedges into wet clay blocks. Sun baking turned mud into unbreakable records. Disputed contracts were literally smashed to dust. Breaking the clay tablet cancelled the agreement. Try smashing your phone over a bad digital bill. See how modern courts react to physical destruction. What happens when laws are written on fragile dirt? Whose contract would survive a hammer blow?
