Why Boxing Rings Are Called Rings

Published 2026-10-16 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You might assume a boxing ring is square. But its name dates back to a circular shape. The term 'ring' is a historical leftover, not a description of its current form. In 18th-century England, fights happened in circles. Spectators formed a 'ring' around the bare-knuckle boxers. The first ropes were added in 1838 to keep fighters in. This shows how tradition can outlast practical changes. Many common phrases carry history in their words, connecting us to past customs and sports.
