Why Do We Still Live in Roman Apartment Designs?

Published 2026-09-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You believe that high-rise apartment living is modern. But ancient Romans lived in crowded wood blocks over two thousand years ago. They called them insulae. These buildings dominated the skyline of Rome. But they were incredibly dangerous to live in. Emperor Augustus passed a law in six BC capping their height at seventy feet maximum because they kept catching fire and collapsing. Try it yourself: walk past a five-story building. That is the exact scale of a standard Roman block. But the top floors had zero running water. Would you live up there without a working toilet? Next up: How Rome built the world's first sewer.
