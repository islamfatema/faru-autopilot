The Aztecs Made School Mandatory Long Before Most of Europe

Published 2026-10-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The Aztecs made school mandatory. In the 1400s, children in the Aztec Empire had to go to school. Not just the rich. Nobles' children went to the calmecac, to learn religion, astronomy and law. Everyone else's went to the telpochcalli, the house of youth. Every neighbourhood had one. In most of Europe, mandatory school came centuries later. Would you have survived Aztec school?
