Air Conditioning Was Invented for Paper Not People

Published 2026-11-02 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think air conditioning was invented to keep people cool. You assume human comfort drove one of modern life's top tech. That common belief is false. Summer heat was not the issue the inventor tried to solve. In 1902, a Brooklyn printer had a major financial disaster. Humidity swelled his paper, making color inks misalign. Willis Carrier built a system to pull moisture from the air. It cooled the room by accident while saving printed pages. Try it yourself: feel the vent blowing cold air in your home. It drops room temperature only by pulling moisture out. Next: early electric cars. Could you survive without AC?
