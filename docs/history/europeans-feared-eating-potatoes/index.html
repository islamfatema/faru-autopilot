Europeans Feared Eating Potatoes

Published 2026-09-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably think potatoes were always a beloved food staple. But when potatoes arrived in Europe in the 16th century, many believed them poisonous. People feared they caused disease and witchcraft, because they grew underground in the dark. It took centuries, and even royal decrees, to convince people to eat them. Now, peel a raw potato. Its bland taste and texture show why it was so easily dismissed and feared. What other foods did we once refuse to eat?
