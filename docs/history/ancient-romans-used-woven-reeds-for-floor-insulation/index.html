Ancient Romans Used Woven Reeds for Floor Insulation

Published 2026-11-10 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Most people believe stone floors remained bare and freezing. Roman homeowners laid thick mats woven from marsh reeds. Plant fibers covered marble flags to trap indoor heat. Hollow air pockets inside stems acted as thermal insulation. Try placing a woven mat on a cold tile floor today. Your feet are experiencing ancient thermal protection.
