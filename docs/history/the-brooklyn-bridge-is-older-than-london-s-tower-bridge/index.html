The Brooklyn Bridge Is Older Than London’s Tower Bridge

Published 2026-09-30 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

London's Tower Bridge looks centuries old. Its gothic design suggests ancient origins. But it's younger than the Brooklyn Bridge. New York's iconic bridge predates London's. The age difference surprises many people. Brooklyn Bridge opened in May of 1883. Its completion was a monumental achievement. Tower Bridge opened eleven years later, in 1894. This meant American engineers led the way, pioneering suspension bridge technology for the world. It showcased American industrial might and innovation.
