Pedestrian Crosswalks Existed in Ancient Pompeii

Published 2026-10-29 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think pedestrian crosswalks are a modern city invention. You assume painted lines began with car traffic. And ancient streets were just lawless dirt tracks. That picture in your head is totally wrong. In the year 79 AD, Pompeii built crosswalks. Engineers placed raised stone blocks across wide avenues. Citizens crossed high above dirty street rainwater. Carriage wheels fit through gaps between the stones. This road layout set standard wagon axle widths worldwide. Try walking across a modern street without painted lines.
