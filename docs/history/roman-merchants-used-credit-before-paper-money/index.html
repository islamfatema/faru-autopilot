Roman Merchants Used Credit Before Paper Money

Published 2026-10-27 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You likely think financial credit is a modern invention. You are completely wrong about ancient payment methods. Roman bankers called *argentarii* managed accounts by 200 BCE. They facilitated money transfers across the entire empire. Promissory notes, *syngraphae*, replaced heavy coin payments. This let merchants trade without carts of physical cash. Try writing an IOU for a friend right now. Consider the inherent trust in that simple piece of paper. That ancient system powered Rome's vast imperial economy.
