Why House Numbers Started With Odd and Even Sides

Published 2026-10-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

In 1805, Paris changed how streets were numbered. People assume addresses always followed logical city patterns. Odd numbers sat proudly on just one side. Even numbers took over the opposite street side. Houses used to get numbers by build date. That earlier chaotic method confused every single traveler. Mail delivery sped up across the growing city. Emergency response times dropped for every single neighborhood. Your package delivery speed depends on old French rules. Finding a new home address would be impossible otherwise.
