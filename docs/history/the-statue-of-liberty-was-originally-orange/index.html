The Statue of Liberty Was Originally Orange

Published 2026-09-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The Statue of Liberty was originally orange. You think it was designed to be green. But when it was built in 1886, it was made of raw, shining copper. It looked like a giant, bright penny. Twenty years of wind and salty sea air oxidized the metal and turned it green. By 1906, the copper was fully hidden. Try this: clean a copper penny with salt and vinegar until it shines bright. That is the statue's original 1886 color. Would it look better if we polished it?
