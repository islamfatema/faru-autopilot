Why Gold and Diamonds Are Measured in Carats

Published 2026-10-17 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You likely assume carats are a lab invention. That scientific belief is entirely incorrect. The term originates in the ancient Mediterranean. Merchants traded goods using carob tree seeds. Carob seeds have an incredibly uniform weight. They worked perfectly as scale counterweights. In 1570, this practice was standardized. Europe locked in carats for precious metals. Ancient botany determines your jewelry value. History dictates the price of modern luxury. Your wealth is measured by ancient agriculture. Would you trust a tree seed with your money?
