Medieval Castles Were Actually Painted Bright Pink

Published 2026-10-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You picture medieval castles as grey stone. Gloomy fortresses built for grim wars. That stark look is a modern illusion. During the Middle Ages, castles were painted. Coated in lime plaster and bright pigments. Lords used vibrant reds and striking pinks. To flaunt their wealth across the valley. Centuries of rain stripped the color away. Leaving behind the bare grey skeleton. Check an old stone wall near you today. Look closely at the mortar between stones. Can you spot traces of ancient pigment?
