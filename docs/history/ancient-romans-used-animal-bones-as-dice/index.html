Ancient Romans Used Animal Bones as Dice

Published 2026-10-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume ancient dice were always square cubes. That assumption is completely wrong. Romans gambled with animal knuckle bones called tali. Sheep and goat ankle bones had four unequal sides. Every single roll depended on unpredictable natural physics. Emperors and soldiers carried these bones everywhere. Try rolling four modern dice and watch them sit flat. Ancient gaming was far wilder than plastic blocks. Your board game night has bizarre ancient roots. Would you bet your wages on a sheep bone?
