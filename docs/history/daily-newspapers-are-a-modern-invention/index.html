Daily Newspapers Are A Modern Invention

Published 2026-10-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably think daily news is an ancient idea. Always delivering the latest events for generations. But truly regular daily newspapers are different. They are a surprisingly recent innovation for us. Not a constant feature throughout most history. The world's first true daily was Germany's in 1650. The *Einkommende Zeitungen* started publishing in Leipzig. Britain's first appeared later, *The Daily Courant* in 1702. For centuries, news was likely days or weeks old. Imagine a life without instant updates today. Our modern world is saturated with constant information. Could you genuinely live without daily news now?
