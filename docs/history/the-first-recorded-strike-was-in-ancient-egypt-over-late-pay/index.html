The First Recorded Strike Was in Ancient Egypt, Over Late Pay

Published 2026-09-13 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

History's first recorded strike was over late pay. Egypt, around 1157 BC. The men who carved the royal tombs hadn't received their grain. Grain was their wages. So they put down their tools and sat outside the temples until they were paid. A scribe wrote it all down. They got their rations - eventually. Three thousand years later, same argument.
