Why US Mailing Addresses Have ZIP Codes

Published 2026-10-28 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

ZIP codes were created by computer programmers. That common assumption is completely wrong. Postal workers sorted mail by hand until 1963. Postwar letter volumes nearly collapsed the system. July 1, 1963 introduced the Zone Improvement Plan. Five digits automated geographical mail sorting. Machines sped up delivery across every state. Your online shipping speed relies on 1963 codes. Postal gridlock was avoided through numeric routing. Are postal codes becoming obsolete with digital routing? Will physical mail disappear in the future?
