The First US Mail Was Dropped From Hot Air Balloons

Published 2026-11-02 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume aerial delivery started with modern cargo jets. You think mailbags only ever traveled by train or truck. In 1859, a pilot launched mail using a giant hot air balloon. John Wise attempted to fly letters across the state of Indiana. The unpredictable wind dropped the cargo into a tree branch. It proved sky mail was entirely at the mercy of weather. Toss a paper envelope out a high window right now. Watch how wind instantly seizes lightweight falling objects. Early aviation experiments were terrifyingly fragile. Would you trust your mail to a drifting canvas bag?
