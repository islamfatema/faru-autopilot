Why Stop Signs Are Shaped Like Octagons

Published 2026-10-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably think stop signs were shaped for visibility. That common belief is completely wrong. In 1923, road signs were officially standardized. The Mississippi Valley Association met to organize traffic. They decided more sides meant higher danger levels. An eight-sided octagon meant a mandatory stop. Drivers approaching from behind could recognize the shape. Even in heavy snow, the outline stood out. This means your daily commute relies on 1923 highway rules. You obey a shape created a century ago. Could modern digital cars replace these octagons soon? What shape would you design instead?
