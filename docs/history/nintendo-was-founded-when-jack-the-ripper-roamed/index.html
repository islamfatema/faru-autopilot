Nintendo Was Founded When Jack the Ripper Roamed

Published 2026-10-26 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think video game giants started with silicon chips. Nintendo began crafting cards in 1889. While Jack the Ripper was still in the news. Fusajiro Yamauchi opened a tiny shop in Kyoto. Making handmade hanafuda cards long before electric lights. Your modern console links straight to gas lamps. And horse-drawn carriages on muddy city streets. Try opening a card deck and picture that era. Before cars or planes ever left the ground. A century-old paper shop built today's gaming empire. What other ancient businesses survive in your room?
