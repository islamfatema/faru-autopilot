Why High Heels Were Originally Worn by Men

Published 2026-09-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

High heels were originally for men. In 10th-century Persia, soldiers wore them. Heels kept their feet secure in stirrups. In 1599, European royals saw the style. They adopted heels to look tall and wealthy. That's the origin of the modern high heel.
