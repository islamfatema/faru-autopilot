Why Parking Meters Were Invented

Published 2026-10-19 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Parking meters are a common sight in our cities. They seem like just another way to collect fees. But their original purpose was far more revolutionary. It wasn't initially about generating city income. They solved a unique urban planning challenge. In 1935, downtown Oklahoma City faced severe congestion. Office workers parked all day, blocking customer access. This caused major problems for local businesses. Meters charged a nickel per hour, forcing turnover. This created available spaces and boosted local commerce. They transformed urban traffic management and city revenue. Could our cities function without parking enforcement today?
