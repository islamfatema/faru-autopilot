Rome Had Water Meters 2000 Years Ago

Published 2026-09-16 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think paying for water is a modern invention. Most people believe ancient water was free. But Roman engineers designed utility billing over two thousand years ago in Rome. To stop wealthy citizens from stealing water, inspectors installed a bronze calix nozzle. It regulated the exact flow size with a seal. Larger unapproved pipes brought massive fines. Turn your kitchen faucet on just a tiny drip, then open it fully to feel the pressure. That opening size is how Rome billed users. Who do you think paid the most for water?
