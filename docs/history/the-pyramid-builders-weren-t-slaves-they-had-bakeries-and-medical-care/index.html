The Pyramid Builders Weren't Slaves - They Had Bakeries and Medical Care

Published 2026-09-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The people who built the pyramids weren't slaves. Archaeologists found their town beside the Giza plateau - with bakeries and kitchens that fed thousands. Their skeletons show broken bones that healed and even amputations they survived - someone was treating them. And they were buried in tombs next to the pyramids, with bread and beer for the afterlife. Slaves don't get buried beside the king. Did you learn in school that slaves built the pyramids?
