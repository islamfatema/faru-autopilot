Russia's Olympic Team Arrived 12 Days Late Because of Its Calendar

Published 2026-09-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Russia's Olympic team arrived twelve days late - because of their calendar. London, 1908. Russia still used the old Julian calendar. Britain used the Gregorian one. By then the two were almost two weeks apart. Russia's shooters planned by their own dates, and turned up after their events were over.
