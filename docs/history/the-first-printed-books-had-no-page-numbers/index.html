The First Printed Books Had No Page Numbers

Published 2026-10-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume every printed book came with page numbers. That belief is totally incorrect. Gutenberg printed his Bible in 1455 without a single digit. Every page was blank at the top and bottom. Early books mimicked handwritten medieval scrolls. Layouts varied wildly between different copies. Readers had to write in their own page numbers. Or pay professional scribes to add them by hand. Pick up a book near you and flip to page fifty. Imagine reading a modern novel with zero tracking guides. What everyday object is missing an obvious feature today?
