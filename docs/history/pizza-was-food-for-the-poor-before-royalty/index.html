Pizza Was Food for the Poor Before Royalty

Published 2026-09-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Many believe pizza was always a global delicacy. That widespread belief is totally wrong. In Naples during 1780, pizza was poor food. Workers ate cheap flatbreads every single day. Queen Margherita visited Naples in 1889. She ate a patriotic pie with three colors. This royal endorsement changed its social status. Peasant food suddenly became a dish for royalty. Global menus now feature this former poverty meal. Your favorite Friday dinner started in slums. Class divides once separated your favorite foods. Would you eat peasant food if royalty made it famous?
