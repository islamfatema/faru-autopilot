Ancient Romans Used Glass Windows in Every House

Published 2026-09-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think glass windows lined every Roman home. You assume clear panes kept the winter cold out. Clear window glass was an extreme luxury item. Reserved only for palaces and grand bathhouses. Ordinary Roman citizens had no transparent glass. They used wooden shutters to block the wind. Or hung heavy woolen cloth over wall openings. Walk to your window and tap the glass now. Imagine living your life without a single pane— how dark would your living room be?
