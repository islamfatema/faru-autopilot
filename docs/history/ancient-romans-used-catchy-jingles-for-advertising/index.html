Ancient Romans Used Catchy Jingles for Advertising

Published 2026-11-11 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think advertising jingles are a modern invention. You assume catchy slogans started with radio or TV. But ancient Romans used them to sell their goods. Street vendors chanted rhymes about their fresh bread. Or gladiatorial matches used memorable phrases. Next time a jingle gets stuck in your head, blame Rome.
