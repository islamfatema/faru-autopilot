Ancient Romans Wore Shoes With Metal Studs

Published 2026-10-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think Roman sandals were soft and quiet. You picture quiet leather walking down dirt roads. That common assumption is completely wrong. Legionaries marched on soles packed with iron hobnails. Dozen of heavy metal studs lined every single boot. It gave soldiers traction in deep mountain mud. It turned every marching step into a crushing weapon. Try stomping on a hard floor in steel-cleated boots today. You are walking with the exact tread of an empire. How did they march thousands of miles on iron soles?
