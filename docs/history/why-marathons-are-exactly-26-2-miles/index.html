Why Marathons Are Exactly 26.2 Miles

Published 2026-10-03 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You know marathons are a grueling 26.2 miles. But the original ancient Greek race was much shorter. It was never set at that precise distance. Its current exact length is due to royal convenience. The 1908 London Olympics solidified the 26.2-mile standard. The British Royal Family requested a specific route change. This added exactly 1.2 miles to the course. Without this royal whim the race would be shorter. Elite runners would face a different physical challenge. Training strategies would need to adapt to a new goal.
