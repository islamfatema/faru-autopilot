Why We Have Standard Time Zones

Published 2026-11-14 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume global time zones were set by governments. That belief is wrong, as local times varied. Before 1883, every town set its own clock. Thousands of different local times existed. This made railroad schedules nearly impossible. Frequent train crashes happened across America. On November 18, 1883, railroads fixed this. They divided the continent into four zones. Permanently changing how we measure time. Can modern society function without synchronized clocks controlling every single daily activity? Think about your schedule.
