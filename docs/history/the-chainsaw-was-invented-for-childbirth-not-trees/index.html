The Chainsaw Was Invented for Childbirth, Not Trees

Published 2026-11-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Chainsaws are built for cutting trees. But they were invented for childbirth. In 1785, C-sections were too dangerous. Doctors cut pelvic bone to help deliver babies. Two Scottish doctors created a hand-cranked saw. It was designed to speed up the surgery. It was only used for trees decades later.
