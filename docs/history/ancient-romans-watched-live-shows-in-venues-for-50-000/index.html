Ancient Romans Watched Live Shows in Venues For 50,000

Published 2026-11-11 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think massive public entertainment venues are modern. Like arenas for concerts or cinemas for films, right? But 2,000 years ago, Romans had venues seating 50,000 people. The Colosseum and Circus Maximus were spectacles. They watched races, plays, and public performances. Their scale rivals any modern stadium or arena. Find a local venue and check its seating capacity. What ancient shows would you want to see resurrected?
