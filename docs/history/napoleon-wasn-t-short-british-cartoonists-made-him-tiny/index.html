Napoleon Wasn't Short - British Cartoonists Made Him Tiny

Published 2026-09-20 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Napoleon wasn't short. He was about 1.69 meters - five foot seven. Average, or a little taller, for a Frenchman of his time. The myth came from two places. French inches were longer than English ones, so his recorded five-foot-two was mistranslated. And British cartoonists drew him tiny on purpose.
