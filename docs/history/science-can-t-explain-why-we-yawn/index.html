Science Can't Explain Why We Yawn

Published 2026-10-25 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You might think science knows everything about your own body. But even a simple action you do every day, like yawning, is still a mystery. Scientists have debated its purpose for centuries. Is it for more oxygen? To cool the brain? Or a social signal for others? No one knows for sure. Try to yawn on command. It’s nearly impossible without seeing someone else yawn first.
