The First Video Game Was Played in 1958

Published 2026-11-04 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think video games started with 70s arcades. The first digital game was built in 1958. A physicist made tennis on a huge laboratory tool. Visitors stood in lines to bounce a single dot. Digital entertainment is older than you realize. Try playing a game on a 1950s lab computer.
