Why We Use Calculators With a 7-8-9 Keypad

Published 2026-10-08 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

All keypads should be like phones, right? With 1, 2, 3 at the very top. Actually, calculator keypads are designed differently. For a very specific, historical reason. It's about optimizing data entry for you. Engineers in the mid-20th C. studied entry speeds. They found 1-2-3 at the bottom was faster. For human hands doing numeric input. This ergonomic design optimizes data entry speed. Affecting efficiency in countless transactions today. It impacts how quickly you can process numbers. Are older designs better for human efficiency?
