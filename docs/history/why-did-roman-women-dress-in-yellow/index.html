Why Did Roman Women Dress in Yellow

Published 2026-11-16 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think Roman women wore white togas daily. That common belief is completely wrong. Brides walked down aisles in blazing saffron yellow. The dye required seventy thousand crocus flowers. Saffron cost more by weight than pure gold. Try it yourself: look at your wedding photos. Did your clothes cost more than an automobile? What status symbols will look absurd tomorrow?
