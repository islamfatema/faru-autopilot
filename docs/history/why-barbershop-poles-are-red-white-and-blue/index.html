Why Barbershop Poles Are Red, White, and Blue

Published 2026-10-17 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think barbershop poles are just festive designs. You believe they relate only to haircuts today. That idea is entirely false and misleading. Medieval barbers were actually trained surgeons. In the Middle Ages they performed real surgeries. They handled bloodletting and pulled aching teeth. The red stripe on poles represented human blood. The white stripe stood for medical bandages. Patients gripped the pole to encourage blood flow. This means your haircut carries medical history. You carry medieval trauma into modern storefronts. How would you feel if your stylist did surgery?
