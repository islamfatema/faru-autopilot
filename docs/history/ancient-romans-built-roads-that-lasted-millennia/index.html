Ancient Romans Built Roads That Lasted Millennia

Published 2026-10-05 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You likely believe modern highways are the best built. That asphalt assumption is entirely mistaken. Roman roads were built with layers of heavy stone. Interlocking basalt blocks lasted two thousand years. Modern asphalt cracks after just one freezing winter. Their deep trenches drained water away from the surface. Stomp your foot on a cracked asphalt street right now. Roman stone paving still outlasts our concrete today. Civil engineering peaked long before modern cars. How many winters will your local street survive?
