The Shortest War in History Lasted About 38 Minutes

Published 2026-09-21 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The shortest war in history lasted about 38 minutes. Zanzibar, 27 August 1896. A new sultan took power without British approval. Britain gave him an ultimatum. When it ran out at nine in the morning, the warships opened fire on the palace. Before ten o'clock, the flag was down and the war was over. Some records say 45 minutes.
