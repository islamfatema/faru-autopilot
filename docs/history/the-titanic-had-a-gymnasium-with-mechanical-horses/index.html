The Titanic Had a Gymnasium With Mechanical Horses

Published 2026-11-12 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The Titanic had an electric gym on the top deck. You picture basic cabins and wooden benches. First-class passengers rode mechanical wooden horses. Electric camels and rowers kept wealthy travelers fit. A ticket bought access to mechanized workouts. The gear sank forever in April 1912. Compare your gym pass to a 1912 steamship.
