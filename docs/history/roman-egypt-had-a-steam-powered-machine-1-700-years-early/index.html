Roman Egypt Had a Steam-Powered Machine 1,700 Years Early

Published 2026-09-14 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Someone built a steam-powered machine in the first century. Hero of Alexandria, Roman Egypt. Water boiled under a metal ball on an axle. Steam shot from two bent nozzles and the ball spun. Then nobody put it to work. One theory: with so much enslaved labour, nobody needed it. The Industrial Revolution was about 1,700 years away.
