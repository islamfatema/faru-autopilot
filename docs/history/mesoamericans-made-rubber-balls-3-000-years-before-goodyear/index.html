Mesoamericans Made Rubber Balls 3,000 Years Before Goodyear

Published 2026-11-02 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think rubber was invented in the 1800s. You credit Goodyear for bouncy balls and tires. That timeline leaves out 3,000 years of chemistry. In 1600 BC, ancient Mesoamericans made rubber. They harvested raw sap from local latex trees. Then mixed it with liquid from morning glory vines. The chemical reaction produced bouncy rubber. Centuries before Europe had a single rubber boot. Ancient technology was far more advanced than taught. Try it yourself: bounce a rubber ball on asphalt. You are testing 3,600-year-old Mesoamerican science.
