The Roman Empire Was Smaller Than Canada

Published 2026-11-08 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The Roman Empire did not cover the globe. You were taught Rome was impossibly gigantic. But comparing real maps changes everything. At its peak in AD 117, Rome reached 5 million km². Modern Canada is nearly twice that total area. Try it yourself: cover one eye and stare at your thumb. Move it sideways—it vanishes. What else were you taught wrong?
