Ancient Romans Wore Rings Made of Pure Iron

Published 2026-10-11 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume status rings have always been shiny gold. You think precious metals were the original Roman symbol. Early Roman citizens wore rings made of raw iron. Hammered from dark metal to signal military grit. Gold was strictly banned for ordinary citizens. Reserved by law only for high ambassadors. Bend a metal paperclip into a ring on your hand. Feel how heavy and industrial iron feels. Every Roman hand carried a badge of raw duty. This strict metal law defined social class. Would you wear an iron ring that rusted on your skin?
