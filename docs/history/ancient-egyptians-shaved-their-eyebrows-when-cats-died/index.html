Ancient Egyptians Shaved Their Eyebrows When Cats Died

Published 2026-10-16 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think mourning pets is a modern emotional habit. In ancient Egypt, the grief was enforced by strict law. When a family cat died, humans shaved their eyebrows. Every single member shaved the hair off their forehead. The historian Diodorus Siculus recorded this rule in 60 BC. It was the universal sign of profound household sorrow. Run your fingertips across your brow right now. Feel how bare that skin feels beneath your touch. Egyptians walked around bald above the eyes for months. Pets held higher legal status than foreign visitors. What modern pet ritual today will sound bizarre tomorrow?
