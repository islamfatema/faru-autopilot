The Surprising 1839 Origin of "OK"

Published 2026-09-28 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

"OK" seems like a universal, timeless word. But this simple term has a surprising secret. Its true origin is quite specific and recent. "OK" began in 1839 as a journalistic joke. Boston newspapers used "o.k." for "oll korrect." This was a playful misspelling of "all correct." An accidental slang term became a global agreement. This shows how quickly language can be shaped. A simple newspaper gag changed how we communicate. Does its chaotic start change your view of "OK"?
