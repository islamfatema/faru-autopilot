The Oldest Recorded Joke Is Nearly 4,000 Years Old - and It's a Fart Joke

Published 2026-09-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

The oldest joke ever recorded is a fart joke. Sumerian, around 1900 BC. Researchers ranked it oldest in 2008. Roughly: something that has never happened since the beginning of time - a young woman who didn't fart in her husband's lap. We don't fully get it. Nearly 4,000 years. Humans have always been this childish. Send this to someone whose jokes are older than they are. number 13: the oldest song with written music. You can still hear it played.
