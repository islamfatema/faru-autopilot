Genghis Khan Built the Largest Land Empire Ever

Published 2026-10-28 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Genghis Khan is famous for his massive empire. He built the largest connected land empire ever. Many believe he created it all in his lifetime. But the true extent was built by his heirs. His successors expanded it hugely after his 1227 death, For almost a century more, the empire grew. This long expansion shaped global history for centuries. It fundamentally changed borders and cultural interactions. The impact reaches our world even today. Should we really credit Genghis for all of it?
