Why Fire Hydrants Are Placed Near Curbs

Published 2026-10-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You see fire hydrants just sitting by the curb. It seems like the most obvious spot. But their exact placement isn't merely accidental. In the 19th century as cities expanded fast, Valves were put at curbs to protect pipes from traffic. And for instant access by horse-drawn fire engines. This simple placement choice saved countless lives then. It shaped how cities were designed for safety. And it impacts our infrastructure to this day.
