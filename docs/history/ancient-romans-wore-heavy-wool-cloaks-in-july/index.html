Ancient Romans Wore Heavy Wool Cloaks in July

Published 2026-11-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You assume Romans wore light clothes for the heat. Citizens wore heavy wool togas in July. Nine pounds of dense fabric baked in the sun. Status mattered far more than physical comfort. Wrap yourself in a thick blanket outside today. Feel how quickly you sweat under the weight. Why do we suffer for fashion just like they did?
