Why Notebook Paper Has Margins

Published 2026-10-18 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You probably think paper margins are for binding. Or maybe for your teacher's red pen marks. Perhaps you assume it is for neatness. But the true reason is far more historical. And it is not what you might expect. In centuries past, rodents were common pests. Mice and rats chewed on outer paper edges. To save valuable writing, margins kept words safe. This ancient problem still shapes modern paper. Today, we inherit design choices from past threats. Even digital documents often maintain similar layouts. Imagine losing crucial text because of a hungry mouse. Should we rethink margins in a digital world?
