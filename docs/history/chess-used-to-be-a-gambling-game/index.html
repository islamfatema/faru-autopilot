Chess Used To Be A Gambling Game

Published 2026-09-09 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think chess is a game of pure skill? No luck involved, just strategy? Well, you've been told wrong for centuries. The earliest form of chess, called Chaturanga, originated in India around 600 AD. And it had dice. DICE. Players rolled them to determine piece movements, making it a game of strategy AND chance. The dice were only removed when it reached Persia. Next time you play chess, imagine rolling the dice. How would it change your game?
