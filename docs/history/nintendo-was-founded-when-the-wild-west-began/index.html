Nintendo Was Founded When the Wild West Began

Published 2026-11-01 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think video game giants started in the digital age. A modern corporate era built on silicon chips. You assume old companies never pivot industries. Nintendo began in 1889 as a small shop in Kyoto. They made handmade cards for traditional games. Cowboys were still roaming the American frontier then. Try holding a deck of cards in your hands tonight. Realize the maker survived the horse-drawn carriage era. A paper card company outlasted entire empires. How did they survive so many centuries of change?
