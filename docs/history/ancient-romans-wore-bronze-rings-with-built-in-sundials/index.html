Ancient Romans Wore Bronze Rings With Built-In Sundials

Published 2026-10-22 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Most people believe watches are modern inventions. That common belief is completely wrong. In 150 AD, wealthy Romans wore miniature bronze rings. Tiny perforated sundials were carved into bands. Holding a hand up showed the current hour. Wearers checked time while walking through forums. Wearable technology existed two thousand years ago. Try it yourself: look at your naked wrist. Guess the exact minute without checking a screen. How close were you to the real time? Could you survive without digital clocks? What wearable tech will we wear next?
