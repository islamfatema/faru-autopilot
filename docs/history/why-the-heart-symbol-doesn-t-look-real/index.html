Why the Heart Symbol Doesn't Look Real

Published 2026-10-07 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think the heart symbol resembles human anatomy. That idea is completely wrong. It comes from Cyrene in the seventh century BC. Merchants grew wealthy trading a wild fennel. Silphium served as medicine and contraception. Its distinctive seed pod formed a familiar shape. Ancient coins featured this exact botanical outline. The shape became linked to romance and desire. A birth control plant invented our symbol for love. Every Valentine card uses an ancient seed shape. Did ancient merchants know their crop would outlive them? Why did a seed become the icon of romance?
