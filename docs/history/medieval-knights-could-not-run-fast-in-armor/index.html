Medieval Knights Could Not Run Fast in Armor

Published 2026-10-11 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Medieval knights could run fast in armor. You think steel suits made them clumsy cans. You were taught they needed cranes to mount. Yet a full suit weighed only fifty pounds total. The weight was spread evenly across joints. Knights could sprint and leap with ease. Try it yourself: put fifty pounds on. Strap a heavy pack on and jog down your street. Feel how balanced a distributed load is. How did movie myths fool us for so long?
