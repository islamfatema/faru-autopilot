Why Royal Purple Was Only For the Rich

Published 2026-09-28 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Purple was seen as the color of royalty. A clear sign of immense wealth and power. But its rarity was not merely a choice. The actual reason was unbelievably high cost. It was due to extreme production difficulty. Ancient Tyrian purple came from murex snails. Over 10,000 snails made just one gram. Making it truly more valuable than gold. This extreme rarity enforced strict sumptuary laws. Only emperors and elite could afford to wear it. Solidifying its role as a symbol of absolute power. Would you pay that much for a color now?
