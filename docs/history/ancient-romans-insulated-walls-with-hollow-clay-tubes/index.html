Ancient Romans Insulated Walls With Hollow Clay Tubes

Published 2026-10-08 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think wall insulation is a modern engineering feat. That common assumption is entirely wrong. Roman villas used hollow tiles inside walls long ago. These terracotta boxes trapped air to block winter dampness. Hot air from floor furnaces flowed right through them. Elite homes stayed warm regardless of the weather. Try tapping on an exterior wall in your house right now. Your rooms rely on thermal principles from antiquity. How did ancient builders grasp physics so clearly?
