How Did Romans Prove Their Identity?

Published 2026-10-08 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think identity theft is a digital problem. But ancient Romans suffered devastating fraud. They did not use passwords or plastic ID cards. Instead, Romans relied on a piece of metal. Your signet ring was your legally binding key. Pliny the Elder wrote in seventy-seven AD about the absolute chaos of stolen rings. A thief with your ring could empty your estate. Try it yourself: write your name in the dark. Your signature changes, but a stolen ring is instant. Would you trust your life to a piece of jewelry?
