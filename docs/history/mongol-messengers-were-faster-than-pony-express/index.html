Mongol Messengers Were Faster Than Pony Express

Published 2026-09-19 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

You think the Pony Express was the fastest? The pinnacle of early long-distance mail? The Mongol Empire had something even better. Centuries before, their Yam system crisscrossed Asia. Messengers covered up to 300 miles a day easily. Through a network of relay stations and fresh horses. This connected an empire from Europe to the Pacific. Imagine walking an hour from your home. Now imagine that distance is a single relay leg. Spanning an entire continent, every single day. What other empires had such hidden sophistication? Next: Mongol Empire's religious tolerance.
