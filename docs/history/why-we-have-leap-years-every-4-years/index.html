Why We Have Leap Years Every 4 Years

Published 2026-09-23 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

Leap years appear to be a simple correction. Just an extra day every four years. Yet the real math is surprisingly complex. It is not always precisely every four years. Centuries needed careful astronomical adjustments. Earth orbits the Sun in 365.25 days. Julius Caesar's calendar of 45 BCE added a day Every four years, but Pope Gregory refined it in 1582. Without these precise adjustments, our seasons Would slowly drift, impacting agriculture and festivals. It keeps our modern life synchronized with nature.
