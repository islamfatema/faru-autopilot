This 3,800-Year-Old Hymn Is Also a Beer Recipe

Published 2026-09-15 · Watch on YouTube

What the video says

This 3,800-year-old hymn is also a beer recipe. It's a Sumerian song to Ninkasi, the goddess of beer, from around 1800 BC. It walks through the steps: baking the bread, soaking the malt, mixing the vat, pouring the beer. Singing it may have helped brewers remember.
