# Setting up AdSense for the three channels

Verified 14 October 2026 against YouTube's own help pages, linked at the
bottom. Nothing here is from a blog.

## The one thing that must not go wrong

**One AdSense account per person.** Not per channel, and not per Google
account. Google matches on payee name, address and phone number, so signing up
again under a different Gmail is still a duplicate.

> "You can only have one AdSense or AdSense for YouTube account under the same
> payee name... Duplicate accounts will not be approved and monetization will
> be turned off for the associated YouTube channel."

The three channels sit under three different Google accounts:

| Channel | Google account |
|---|---|
| History That Explains the World | islamfatema596@gmail.com |
| FaRu Fact | lovetocode2010@gmail.com |
| Rise With Fate | islamfatema04@gmail.com |

So the obvious move - set up AdSense on each one - is the move that gets all
three disabled. **One account, three channels linked to it.** YouTube supports
this explicitly: one AdSense for YouTube account can monetize more than one
channel, because the link is to the channel, not to a Google login.

## Which Google account to put it on

**lovetocode2010@gmail.com.** It only has to be an account that will still
exist and still be reachable in five years, because every payment for every
channel goes through it.

**This is not the same question as which channel to start from.** The sign-up
has a step that asks which Google account to use for AdSense, so you can begin
in any channel's Studio and still put the account on lovetocode2010. The two
choices are independent:

| | choose |
|---|---|
| which channel's Studio to start in | whichever one actually shows the card - try History first |
| which Google account holds AdSense | always lovetocode2010@gmail.com |

Start with **History** (islamfatema596@gmail.com). YouTube shows the sign-up
card as a channel comes into range, and History has 131 subscribers against
FaRu Fact's 24 - if the card is anywhere, it is there first.

## Step 1 - create it, once

1. Sign in to <https://studio.youtube.com/> as **islamfatema596@gmail.com**
   (History) - or whichever channel shows the card
2. Left menu → **Earn**
3. Find the card **"Sign-up for AdSense for YouTube"** → **START**
4. Re-enter the password when asked
5. When it asks which Google account to use for AdSense, choose
   **"Use a different account"** and sign in as **lovetocode2010@gmail.com**
6. Confirm the email shown at the top of the page reads lovetocode2010
7. Add contact details and submit

Approval takes a few days and arrives by email. The Earn card then shows the
account as approved and active.

**If the card is not there yet:** stop, and do not go and create an account at
adsense.google.com instead. The card appears as the channel comes into range,
and an account created down the wrong path is the duplicate this whole page is
about. Nothing is lost by waiting - AdSense approval is days, not weeks.

## Step 2 - link the other two to the same account

For the two channels you did not start from - so **lovetocode2010@gmail.com**
(FaRu Fact) and **islamfatema04@gmail.com** (Rise), if you began on History:

1. Sign in to <https://studio.youtube.com/> as that account
2. Left menu → **Earn**
3. Start the AdSense step, and when it asks which Google account to use,
   sign in as **lovetocode2010@gmail.com** - the one that already holds the
   AdSense account
4. It should show the existing account rather than offering to create one. If
   it offers to create a new one, do not continue - that is the duplicate

A linked AdSense account can only be changed **once every 32 days**, so it is
worth getting right the first time rather than fixing it later.

## What to have ready

- Legal name exactly as it appears on tax documents - this is the payee name
  Google matches duplicates on, so it must be identical every time
- Postal address
- Phone number

Tax details (a W-9 for a US person) and bank details are asked for later, at
the payments step, not at sign-up. Payment itself only happens after the
channel is accepted into the Partner Programme and passes the $100 threshold,
with an address PIN verification at $10 earned.

## Why do this before the thresholds are met

It is the one requirement that is not gated on subscribers or watch hours, and
approval takes days. Left until the numbers are reached, it adds that wait on
top of the Partner Programme review. Done now, it is simply already done.
